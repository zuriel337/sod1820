## 4. What the compiler now enforces

Every registration must declare:

- stable `surfaceId` and route family;
- canonical subject types;
- semantic and presentation owners;
- canonical availability owner/state/flag;
- public/private/internal, locale and access classes;
- SEO/indexability/canonical/share/crawler decisions;
- meaningful and expensive semantic actions;
- participation in Reality Graph, Research Context, Raziel and Personal Reality;
- telemetry, trace/cost, release SHA and before/after obligations;
- privacy class, Attention owner, acceptance fixtures and evidence references.

The compiler fails on duplicate surface IDs or route ownership. It also rejects content, findings, graph rows, personal data and visual payloads so the self-model cannot silently become a parallel truth, graph, personal-data or design system.

`OPEN` is intentionally strict: rendering is insufficient. Telemetry, deployed-SHA attribution and before/after measurement are mandatory.
