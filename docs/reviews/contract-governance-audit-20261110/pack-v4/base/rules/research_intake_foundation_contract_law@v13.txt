[UPDATE v12 · Human-Gate ZURIEL · 20.9.2026 — SOURCE MEDIA FIDELITY / STRUCTURAL VISUAL EVIDENCE · EXTEND_EXISTING]

12.1 SOURCE MEDIA CAN BE LOAD-BEARING. When a research claim, method, spatial arrangement, diagram, geometry, matrix, 2D/3D construction, annotation, color/position relationship, handwritten layout or other meaning depends materially on an image/video/document page, that media is part of the Source/Research Artifact. It MUST remain addressable through the existing source/media identity and provenance. It is not a decorative attachment.

12.2 SOURCE ≠ OCR ≠ CAPTION ≠ ANALYSIS ≠ DERIVATIVE RENDER. OCR, transcription, captioning, AI visual description, extracted labels, reconstructed geometry, generated 3D scene/model and human/system interpretation are derivative layers. None replaces the original media. If the source image is missing, the extraction is incomplete even when OCR/text survives.

12.3 COMPOUND SOURCE CONTEXT. A WhatsApp/post/message may carry text + image/video together. Preserve the relationship between the source occurrence and each representation. The wording and media may jointly establish meaning; do not silently detach the image and treat the remaining text as the whole source. Each media representation still retains its own identity/placement semantics under reality_graph_law.

12.4 STRUCTURAL VISUAL EXTRACTION. For visual research whose meaning depends on structure, extraction MAY preserve typed observations such as visible labels, shape/polyhedron/matrix form, axes, adjacency, ordering, relative position, repetition/multiplicity, arrows/lines, highlighted regions, dimensions and source-authored annotations. These are observations/extractions with provenance, not automatic proof of the contributor's interpretation.

12.5 3D / SPATIAL RECONSTRUCTION IS DERIVED AND REPLAYABLE. A later SVG/2D/3D/VR representation of a source diagram is a derivative representation over the same Research Artifact, not a new truth object. It must retain source-media reference plus enough transformation/method provenance to explain how the rendering was obtained. Renderer geometry never upgrades verification/canonicality.

12.6 OCR IS SEARCH/EXTRACTION SUPPORT, NOT MEDIA IDENTITY. OCR equality or textual similarity never proves that two images are the same artifact. Media identity/dedup follows reality_graph_law strong/candidate/supporting evidence rules. Do not merge media automatically from OCR text, filename, topic or visual similarity alone.

12.7 FAILURE/INCOMPLETENESS MUST BE HONEST. If media exists but cannot be fetched, decoded, OCR'd, transcribed or structurally interpreted, preserve the source/media reference and record an explicit incomplete/needs-analysis state. Do not mark the research source fully extracted merely because the textual caption was processed.

12.8 TOPIC / RESEARCH PROJECTION REFERENCES MEDIA, IT DOES NOT COPY IT. When a governed Topic/Convergence includes a load-bearing source image, the projection references the existing source/media artifact and, subject to access/publication rules, may render: (A) original media/source; (B) contributor wording; (C) OCR/transcription where useful; (D) system research analysis/verification; (E) derived structural/3D representation where available. These layers remain distinguishable.

12.9 CONTRIBUTOR EXAMPLE IS CALIBRATION, NOT SPECIAL ARCHITECTURE. Zvi/OPOC 3D-gematria material demonstrates the need: examples such as a source-authored 'כתר בגימטריא תלת מימדית' with an accompanying image must preserve the image together with the written explanation and later analysis. The same contract applies to every contributor/source.

12.10 STORAGE/PLACEMENT. Reuse existing carriers such as channel_updates.image_url, protected/private media refs, gallery/media identities and research_objects provenance/meta adapters where applicable. Storage shape is implementation. No new Media Store, Image Store, 3D Store, Topic Store or contributor-specific system is authorized by this update.

12.11 TRUTH / ACCESS. Media preservation, OCR success or structural richness does not canonicalize, verify or publish a claim. Verification, governance, privacy/access and publication remain separate Truth axes and Human-Gate controlled.

[UPDATE v13 · Human-Gate ZURIEL · 20.9.2026 — SOURCE / BINARY MEDIA RETENTION + COST CONTROL · EXTEND_EXISTING]

13.1 SOURCE RECORD != BINARY PAYLOAD. Retention of a source occurrence (text, attribution, time, channel, source identity/provenance and replay metadata) is a separate operational decision from retention of the heavy image/video/audio/document bytes attached to it. KEEP_SOURCE never means “keep every binary forever”.

13.2 LOAD-BEARING ORIGINALS REMAIN PROTECTED. If §12 determines that original media is load-bearing for a claim, geometry, spatial arrangement, handwritten annotation, exact witness, OCR dispute, source interpretation or replay, the original remains PROVENANCE_PROTECTED and addressable. OCR, caption, thumbnail, transcript or derivative render does not replace it.

13.3 RESEARCH ATTENTION != SOURCE RETENTION. A source classified reviewed_no_structured_findings / source-only / no-research-signal may disappear from default Research/Convergence worklists while its source occurrence remains addressable. “Not research-worthy” is not a deletion authorization. Spam/chatter may be operationally deprioritized without manufacturing a Finding and without erasing source history.

13.4 MEDIA DISPOSITION PROFILE — logical projection, NOT a DB enum/store:
- KEEP_ORIGINAL — original bytes are load-bearing, actively published/featured, required for exact replay, or otherwise proven necessary.
- KEEP_SOURCE_LIGHT — source occurrence/provenance remains; heavy original may be retired only after the minimum media tombstone/fingerprint package is durably preserved under an existing owner and no live reader/reference requires the original.
- ARCHIVE_BINARY — move the same media identity to a cheaper/archive tier while preserving resolvable identity/provenance and authorized retrieval; storage provider/location is not semantic identity.
- PURGE_BINARY_CANDIDATE — only non-load-bearing, unreferenced, non-active binary after complete dependency/runtime/publication proof and successful dry run.
- HUMAN_REVIEW — unknown dependency, missing identity/fingerprint carrier, ambiguous source value, or any destructive uncertainty.
This profile is orthogonal to §11 row-retention classes PROVENANCE_PROTECTED / ACTIVE_SOURCE / BOUNDED_RUNTIME / PURGE_CANDIDATE / HUMAN_REVIEW.

13.5 KEEP_SOURCE_LIGHT MINIMUM PROVENANCE. Before heavy bytes can be removed, an existing owner must preserve enough durable information to prove that media existed and to explain the disposition: source occurrence identity; original/source locator when meaningful; stable media/object identity when one exists; content hash/fingerprint where available; MIME/type; byte size where measurable; original/derived relationship; retained thumbnail/OCR/transcript references where applicable; disposition reason; decision/executor provenance and time. If current carriers cannot preserve this without a broken or misleading image_url/reference, purge FAILS CLOSED to ARCHIVE_BINARY/HUMAN_REVIEW. This clause authorizes no new Media/Retention Store; a later implementation may EXTEND_EXISTING source/media owners only after owner check.

13.6 ACTIVE PRODUCT USE BLOCKS PURGE. Media currently rendered by Broadcasts/Contributor/Gallery/Post/Topic/World or another live authorized surface, used as featured media, or required by a current ingestion/OCR/transcription workflow cannot be purged merely because its source text was already extracted. Reader/runtime dependency is part of the dry-run proof.

13.7 PHYSICAL DEDUP != SOURCE MERGE. Strong content identity may allow one physical binary to serve several source occurrences, but each source occurrence keeps its own source/provenance identity, attribution and context. Similar filename/OCR/visual appearance is not sufficient identity evidence. Dedup may save bytes; it may not collapse research/source history.

13.8 DERIVATIVES ARE SEPARATE. Regenerable thumbnails, transcoded previews and other derivatives may have shorter bounded retention when their owner proves deterministic/regenerable lineage and no surface directly requires that exact derivative. Deleting a derivative never implies permission to delete its original; deleting an original never follows merely from having a derivative.

13.9 CONTROL PLANE / MAINTENANCE PROJECTION. Existing admin_retention_preview/admin_system_health should eventually project, from owner-native data and without a new store: source/media row counts; original/derivative bytes; protected/active/archive/purge-candidate/unknown bytes; research-linked/load-bearing count; active-surface blockers; orphan/duplicate candidates; oldest/newest; largest objects; projected reclaimable bytes; and reasons/blocking references. UNKNOWN bytes stay UNKNOWN and are never counted as savings.

13.10 TWO-PHASE BINARY CLEANUP. Binary cleanup follows §11.5: PREVIEW/DRY RUN first, then a separately Human-Gated executor. The executor must be reference-aware, idempotent, auditable, privacy-safe, recoverable/rollback-aware, and must re-check dependencies immediately before mutation. It must not leave live product URLs silently broken. A row cleanup and a binary cleanup are separate operations and may be approved independently.

13.11 NO ARBITRARY RETENTION CLOCK. This contract does not invent 30/90/180-day deletion windows. Future duration policy is set per operational/media class only after measured growth/cost, legal/privacy/audit needs, replay requirements and live-reader dependencies are proven. Age is an input to review, never authority by itself.

13.12 STORAGE TIERING IS ALLOWED, IDENTITY CHANGE IS NOT. Supabase Storage, R2 or another future provider may be selected for cost/performance by the media/operations implementation as long as identity, provenance, access control, replay and source linkage survive. Moving bytes is not a new Source, Finding or publication event.

13.13 GOVERNED SELF-MAINTENANCE INTEGRATION. system_suggestions/system-watchman may detect storage growth, oversized binaries, missing derivatives, orphan candidates, high egress or archive opportunity and recommend a bounded action with observed evidence and estimated savings. Per system_suggestions_law v3, destructive data changes are never self-applied. Acceptance may prepare a branch/preview/executor plan; production purge remains separately Human-Gated/release-gated.
