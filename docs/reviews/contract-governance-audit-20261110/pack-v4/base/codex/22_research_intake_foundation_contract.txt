## 2. SOURCE / CITATION SEMANTICS

`source` (text) — **ערוץ/מקור-המקור** של הטענה (לדוגמה: `'discovery-engine'`, `'wa-channel'`, `'מערכת כי לה׳ המלוכה'`, שם-תורם). ללא-שינוי מהשימוש הקיים.

`source_ref` (text) — **הציטוט הראשי/היחיד** לטענה (slug של פוסט, מזהה הודעת-וואטסאפ, מראה-מקום פסוק, URL). מכאן ואילך: **הייצוג הקנוני לציטוט-בודד**.

`meta.source_refs` (jsonb array) — **לא כפילות למחוק** (כפי שה-Challenge ביקש לא-להניח). סמנטיקה מדויקת: כאשר יש **יותר מציטוט אחד** לאותה טענה (למשל 3 מקורות-בלתי-תלויים מתכנסים על אותו קשר) — `source_ref` נושא את הציטוט **הראשי**, ו-`meta.source_refs[]` נושא את **שאר** הציטוטים התומכים. אם יש ציטוט יחיד — `source_ref` בלבד, בלי `meta.source_refs`.

**Canonical representation מכאן-ואילך:** `source_ref`=ראשי (יחיד-או-ראשון), `meta.source_refs[]`=תוספת-בלבד-כשיש-ריבוי. לא נוצר מנגנון שלישי.

**Extension point (לא-נבנה כרגע):** `Source`/`Book`/`Edition`/`Textual Version` — כשיידרשו כישויות-first-class, ייוצגו כ-`nodes` (`type='source'`/`'book'`/`'edition'`) לפי `entity_types`/`unified_graph_law` הקיימים — **לא** טבלה חדשה. `source_ref` יצביע-אליהם ברגע-שיקודמו. **הבהרה (Article Stress Test #3, 27.8.2026, לא-חוק-חדש):** claim מיקום-טקסטואלי (למשל "המילה ה-787 בפרשה") אינו-בר-שחזור-בטוח בלי corpus+book/section+edition+**tokenizer/version**+**counting convention**+position — כל-אלה נכנסים **תחת-אותו** Extension Point שכבר-הוגדר-כאן (Source/Book/Edition/Textual Version), לא-קטגוריה-חדשה: ברגע-שיוקם node מסוג `edition`, `meta.ext.text_position.{tokenizer,tokenizer_version,counting_convention}` נספח-אליו לפי-דפוס-`meta.ext.<domain>.<key>` הקיים (§1) — **ALREADY COVERED / CLARIFICATION ONLY**, אין בונים tokenizer בפאס-הזה.

## 6. CONTRIBUTOR SCOPE & CORPUS COMPLETENESS (stress-test derived, 26.8.2026)

**מקור:** נגזר מ-2 Corpus Stress Tests עצמאיים (Track A — צבי: WhatsApp/3D-Geometry corpus; Track עמית — Existing Corpus: media-archive multi-lingual/writer-method corpus), שנועדו לבחון אם החוזה מסעיפים 1-5 מחזיק גם על corpus/חוקר/מקור שונים לגמרי בלי redesign. **תוצאת המבחן: כן — בתנאי שמוסיפים את 6 העקרונות הבאים, כולם מנוסחים מעל primitives קיימים בלבד (0 טבלה/מנוע/schema חדשים).**

### 6.1 Contributor Scope Separation Law
כל ממצא הנוגע לתורם (contributor) מסווג תחילה לאחת משתי קטגוריות נבדלות, לפני כל עיבוד נוסף:
- **ABOUT-CONTRIBUTOR** → שייך לדוסייה של התורם (`contributors` + `contributors.dossier_settings`) — מידע-זהות/ביוגרפי/מטא על האדם עצמו.
- **BY-CONTRIBUTOR-ABOUT-WORLD** → מועמד למחקר אוניברסלי (`research_objects` עם `meta.contributor_id`) — תוכן/טענה/ממצא שהתורם יצר אודות העולם/גימטריה/מקורות, ולא אודות עצמו.
שני הסוגים יכולים לשבת על אותה שורת-מקור (למשל הודעת WhatsApp אחת) — הסיווג הוא ברמת ה-תוכן, לא ברמת ה-שורה. אין הנחת-ברירת-מחדל ששורה שלמה שייכת לסוג אחד (פער ידוע — ראו בסוף הסעיף).

### 6.2 Source-Window / Corpus Completeness Law
הצהרת "corpus מוצה" (exhausted) אסורה ללא ראיה מפורשת, ומדורגת בשלוש רמות נבדלות שאסור לערבב:
- **SOURCE EXHAUSTED** — כל הפריטים הידועים ממקור בודד ומזוהה (קובץ-ייצוא WhatsApp אחד, ערוץ אחד) נסרקו.
- **KNOWN CORPUS EXHAUSTED** — כל המקורות הידועים-כרגע לתורם נסרקו (אך ייתכנו מקורות לא-ידועים/לא-מחוברים עדיין).
- **CONTRIBUTOR CORPUS COMPLETE** — אין שום ראיה/אינדיקציה למקור נוסף כלשהו (הרמה הגבוהה ביותר, נדירה).
כל דיווח-סטטוס-מיצוי חייב לנקוב באיזו משלוש הרמות מדובר.

### 6.3 Representation Collapse Law
אסור "לקפל" ישות-מקור ישירות לכדי ממצא-מאומת. השרשרת המחייבת:
**SOURCE ARTIFACT** (הודעה/תמונה/קובץ גולמי) → **EXTRACTED CONTENT** (טקסט/OCR/תמלול שחולץ ממנו) → **CORE FINDING / CLAIM** (הטענה שהכותב מעלה) → **EVIDENCE / REPRESENTATIONS** (הצגות שונות של אותה טענה: גימטריה בעברית, תרגום, ציור-גיאומטרי, טבלה).
כל שלב נשמר בנפרד ומקושר (`source_ref`, `meta.contributor_id`, `provenance_type`) — לא מוחלף. ריבוי ייצוגים של אותו CORE FINDING (למשל עברית+אנגלית, או טקסט+קוביה גיאומטרית) אינם ממצאים נפרדים ואינם כפילות.

### 6.4 Access Is Orthogonal To Truth/Scope Law
רמת-גישה (`privacy_scope`, `dossier_settings.visibility`, `sensitive=true`) היא ציר נפרד לגמרי מציר-האמת (CLAIM/VERIFIED/CANONICAL) ומציר ה-Scope (§6.1). תוכן פרטי/רגיש יכול להיות מאומת-מנוע (`engine_verified=true`) בלי להיות ציבורי (מרחיב את §5 PRIVATE CANONICAL≠PUBLIC). אין להסיק "לא ציבורי ⇐ פחות אמין", ואין להסיק "מאומת ⇐ מותר לפרסם". שינוי-גישה הוא תמיד פעולה נפרדת ומפורשת בשער-אנושי — לעולם לא תוצר-לוואי של אימות/סיווג.

### 6.5 No Contributor-Specific Engine Law
אין לבנות מנוע/פענוח/טבלה/ניקוד ייחודי לתורם בודד (למשל מנוע ייעודי לשיטת-כתיבה אישית, או מנוע-גיאומטריה ייעודי לתורם אחד). שיטת-כתיבה ייחודית לתורם (writer-method / WRITER_DECLARED_PATTERN) נשמרת כ-metadata מתועד (`meta.ext.writer_method.<name>`) ולא כלוגיקת-מנוע. קידום שיטה כזו לשיטה קנונית עובר תמיד דרך `agent_onboarding_law`/`method_priority` הרגילים (הגדרות נעולות של צוריאל), לא דרך קוד ספציפי-לתורם. מרחיב את `els_single_engine_law`/`gematria_engine_law` לכל דומיין-חוקר.

### 6.6 Exhaustion Before Freeze Law
הכרזת Contract Freeze על "חוזה חילוץ-מחקר אוניברסלי" אסורה לפני שלפחות N≥3 corpora שונים-במהות (חוקר שונה, מבנה-מקור שונה, שפה/שיטה שונה) עברו stress-test מלא ותועדו. **עודכן 27.8.2026: 3/3 הושלמו** — (1) צבי — Track A (WhatsApp/contributor-corpus, spatial/3D); (2) עמית — Existing Corpus (media-archive, multilingual/writer-method, private-contributor); (3) **Article 145** (`posts.id=145`, wp_id=31656, "סוד החשמל" — פוסט-מפורסם-ארוך, `source='wordpress'`, ציטוטים-מקראיים, ריבוי-שיטות-גימטריה, נגזרות-מתמטיות, תמונות, claims גיאומטריים/מיקום-טקסטואלי, midrash — **שונה-מהותית**: לא-קורפוס-תורם-פרטי אלא תוכן-עורכי-מפורסם). כל-שלושת ה-stress-tests הניבו **0 MUST FOUNDATION NOW** (ר' §6-FREEZE למטה לפירוט-Article-145). **N≥3 מתקיים — ר' §6-FREEZE להכרזת-הסגירה הפורמלית.**

**סיווג 6 העקרונות (Foundation Expansion Gate):** כולם **EXTENSION POINT NOW** — מנוסחים מעל primitives קיימים בלבד (`contributor_id`, `source_ref`, `privacy_scope`, `meta.ext.<domain>.<key>`, §5), 0 schema חדש. אינם MUST FOUNDATION NOW (לא חוסמים עבודה קיימת) ואינם LATER (כבר נחוצים לפאס-הבא ב-stress-test).

**פערים ידועים (מדווחים בכנות, לא נסגרים בפאס הזה):**

## 7. EXTRACTION INTEGRITY PATCH — ZVI 3060 GOLDEN RECONSTRUCTION (27.8.2026)

**Human-Gate:** ZURIEL explicitly ordered correction of the extraction contract after the 3060 reconstruction. This section is additive and supersedes only the earlier conclusion that the Zvi spatial specimen exposed 0 MUST-FOUNDATION-NOW extraction gaps. The historical Freeze text remains as provenance.

### 7.1 Semantic Operand / Quantity Provenance Law — MUST FOUNDATION NOW
An extracted arithmetic result is incomplete when a literal operand/quantity came from source meaning outside the calculation engine and that origin is lost. Every externally supplied operand/quantity that materially participates in a derivation must preserve **why this number is present**, not only its numeric value.

Required semantic fields may live under existing flexible metadata (no schema change), for example `meta.ext.derivation.inputs[]`: `value`, `role`, `origin_type`, `origin_ref`/`origin_statement`, and verification/provenance state. `origin_type` may describe a source-declared quantity, corpus count, geometric count, textual position, temporal quantity, or another source-backed semantic origin. Domain-specific vocabularies belong to their domain adapter; Intake owns the universal requirement that the origin survive.

Golden specimen: source says a cube has six faces and each face is `ישר=510`, producing `6×510=3060`. Preserving only `ישר=510` or only `6×510=3060` is an extraction-integrity failure if the fact that **6 means cube faces** is lost. Engine verification of arithmetic does not verify the semantic origin of the quantity.

### 7.2 Source Media Reference Preservation Law — MUST FOUNDATION NOW
A source artifact that explicitly references media must preserve the media reference as provenance even when the binary asset or URL cannot currently be resolved. `image_url IS NULL` does **not** mean “no image existed” when the source text contains markers such as `[Image 3848.jpg]`.

New extraction must preserve, through existing metadata extension space (no new table required), the raw media marker/reference, source-artifact identity, resolution state (`resolved`/`unresolved`/`missing_asset`), and resolved URL/id when available. An unresolved marker is evidence of a missing attachment, not permission to silently drop the representation.

Golden specimens: Zvi 3060 source rows contain `[Image 3848.jpg]`, `[Image 3850.jpg]`, `[Image 3855.jpg]`, `[Image 3856.jpg]` while `channel_updates.image_url` is NULL. The extraction must retain those references and explicitly mark them unresolved until reconciled.

### 7.3 Extraction Fidelity Gate
For complex sources, `ENGINE_VERIFIED` arithmetic is not sufficient evidence that extraction succeeded. Before declaring a source slice successfully extracted, verify that materially relevant roles survived across: SOURCE ARTIFACT → EXTRACTED CONTENT → PROCEDURE/DERIVATION → FINDING/CLAIM → EVIDENCE/REPRESENTATIONS.

The gate specifically checks: semantic operand origins, media/source anchors, operation order/dependency, and distinct representations. Loss of any materially claim-bearing element is `EXTRACTION_INCOMPLETE`, even when all surviving calculations are correct.

### 7.4 Spatial boundary
This patch does **not** define Spatial Gematria domain laws and does not create a geometry engine/store/graph. Spatial-specific identity, region, containment, boundary/interior, face/vertex semantics remain the responsibility of the existing single Spatial capability (`spatialModels.js` and its future adapter). The only universal Intake rule added here is: when such semantics supply an operand or representation, Intake must not erase their provenance.

The v5 Freeze remains historical provenance for the contract state reached before the 3060 Golden Reconstruction. For **complex extraction integrity**, that conclusion is superseded: `FOUNDATION PATCH REQUIRED / CONTRACT v6`. Controlled ingestion of already-covered simple shapes may continue, but further complex spatial/media ingestion must not be treated as extraction-complete until §7.1–§7.3 are implemented and the 3060 golden specimens pass end-to-end.

## 10. CONTEXTUAL SOURCE GAP / BOOK ACQUISITION ALERT — Human-Gate ZURIEL · 10.9.2026

OWNER CHECK=EXTEND_EXISTING. This extends Research Intake Source/Book/Edition provenance only. No new source engine, book registry, store, graph or truth layer.

When an active research thread reaches a claim/family that is insufficiently anchored in books already present in the canonical system, the Research OS should produce a CONTEXTUAL SOURCE GAP recommendation. The sequence is: (1) search existing Book/Source identities and exact loci first; (2) if existing material is insufficient, recommend missing books/sources; (3) for each recommendation preserve the specific claim/family it is expected to anchor, why it is relevant, priority P1/P2/P3, whether a Book/Witness/Digital Object is already present, and current acquisition/adjudication state; (4) notify/surface the recommendation to ZURIEL in the active research context and future research-status views; (5) when an adequate source is ingested and exact locus is verified, mark the gap resolved/superseded rather than deleting history.

Truth boundary: SOURCE GAP ≠ SOURCE FACT ≠ BOOK AUTHORITY ≠ CANONICAL. A recommended book is a research acquisition candidate only. AI may propose and rank sources; it may not auto-ingest, assign source-of-record authority, canonicalize, publish, or claim that a book supports a statement before an exact locus is verified. Human Gate remains ZURIEL.

Ranking should prefer decision-changing sources: primary/canonical domain sources before secondary summaries; exact direct treatment before generic thematic similarity; already-owned/available witnesses before unnecessary acquisition; and sources that can close multiple active gaps may rank higher, with the reason shown. No black-box score.

Recommended projection fields (logical, no schema mandate): research_scope/topic; claim_or_family; existing_source_hits[]; missing_source_candidates[{book,reason,target_claim,priority,availability_state,witness_state}]; gap_state {open,partially_anchored,resolved,superseded}; exact_locus_required=true; provenance. ## §11 — 2029 SOURCE RETENTION / CLEANUP CONTROL (Human-Gate ZURIEL · 17.9.2026)

**OWNER CHECK: EXTEND_EXISTING Research Intake + existing Privacy/Truth/domain operational owners.** No Retention Store, cleanup registry, archive DB or parallel source system is created.

### 11.1 RETENTION IS NOT TRUTH
Retention/deletion eligibility is an operational lifecycle decision. It never changes epistemic type, verification, governance, canonicality, publication or Research Strength.

### 11.2 PROVENANCE-PROTECTED BEFORE AGE-BASED CLEANUP
A row is not eligible for automatic purge merely because it is old, processed, hidden or terminal. Before deletion the system must prove that the row is not required by any canonical/source provenance, Research Object/source_ref, evidence/decision lineage, graph/source relation, publication/audit requirement, media/context thread, person/contributor attribution, exact replay or active runtime reader. Unknown dependency = HUMAN_REVIEW, not safe-to-delete.

### 11.3 LOGICAL RETENTION CLASSES
These are control-plane classifications, not a new DB enum:
- `PROVENANCE_PROTECTED` — referenced source/evidence/replay/history; retain while dependency exists.
- `ACTIVE_SOURCE` — still-written or live product/source surface; retain under domain owner.
- `BOUNDED_RUNTIME` — operational log/queue/metadata that may receive a bounded retention window once readers and audit needs are proven.
- `PURGE_CANDIDATE` — terminal/unreferenced operational material after a dependency-safe dry run.
- `HUMAN_REVIEW` — ambiguous or destructive cleanup; no automatic deletion.

### 11.4 INITIAL WHATSAPP/INGRESS RETENTION CALIBRATION
- `channel_updates`: PROVENANCE_PROTECTED when referenced; ACTIVE_SOURCE while live/published/used by broadcast/media runtime. Unreferenced expired material may be reviewed later, never blanket-purged.
- `wa_bot_log`: PROVENANCE_PROTECTED for rows referenced by Research OS or needed for historical contributor/person timelines; remaining raw chatter may become BOUNDED_RUNTIME only after dependency audit.
- `wa_deep_queue`: currently terminal rows are **not yet purge-safe** because live historical timeline code reads the table; classify HUMAN_REVIEW/COMPATIBILITY until that dependency is migrated or explicitly retired.
- `wa_vip_inbox`: KEEP_SOURCE / PROVENANCE_PROTECTED by default because it is raw VIP-author intake feeding phrase extraction and attribution; retirement requires proof that downstream typed provenance can replay the source.
- `wa_msg_ext`: BOUNDED_RUNTIME candidate (metadata/dedup only, no message text), but retention duration is not guessed here.
- `wa_message_status`: PURGE_CANDIDATE only for terminal, unreferenced delivery state after dry-run dependency proof; duration set by its operational owner.
- `wa_publish_log`: operational/audit history; bounded retention only after audit/replay requirements are resolved.

### 11.5 TWO-PHASE CLEANUP — PREVIEW THEN PURGE
Every destructive cleanup path must first produce a **Dry Run / Preview** showing table, candidate count, date/status reason, bytes/rows where measurable, protected/reference count, unknown-dependency count and representative samples/IDs. Physical deletion is a separate authorized action. Dry-run eligibility never equals deletion authorization.

### 11.6 CONTROL CENTER PROJECTION
The future Admin/Command Center may project retention health from existing tables/adapters: current rows/size, growth, retention class, protected vs candidate counts, oldest/newest, why-protected, and cleanup preview. The UI is a projection only; it does not become a Retention truth store. It should support: `Preview cleanup`, `keep longer`, `exclude source/contributor`, `show blockers/references`, and an explicit destructive confirmation path governed by current Human-Gate/security rules.

### 11.7 NO AUTOMATIC PURGE UNTIL EXECUTOR ACCEPTANCE
This contract authorizes classification and dry-run design only. It does **not** authorize deleting current rows, installing an automatic purge cron, or choosing arbitrary 30/90/180-day windows. A future executor must prove reference-awareness, idempotency, negative tests, auditability, privacy compliance, and recovery/backup strategy before any automatic cleanup is enabled.

## §13 — SOURCE / BINARY MEDIA RETENTION + COST CONTROL
**Human-Gate ZURIEL · 20.9.2026 · OWNER CHECK=EXTEND_EXISTING**

### 13.1 Two independent retention decisions
A Source occurrence and its heavy binary payload are not the same retention object. Text, source identity, contributor/channel/time provenance and replay metadata may remain while original image/video/audio/document bytes follow a separately governed media disposition. KEEP_SOURCE does not mean “keep every binary forever”.

### 13.2 Load-bearing original
Where §12 makes original media load-bearing for the research/source meaning, exact witness, geometry, annotation, OCR dispute or replay, the original is PROVENANCE_PROTECTED. OCR/transcript/thumbnail/AI description/derived 3D are derivatives and never substitute automatically for the original.

### 13.3 No-research-signal material
reviewed_no_structured_findings, source-only chatter or spam may be removed from default Research/Convergence worklists while the source occurrence remains addressable. Research attention is a projection; exclusion from research is not deletion and does not manufacture a Finding.

### 13.4 Logical media disposition profile
This is a projection vocabulary, not a DB enum or new store:
- KEEP_ORIGINAL
- KEEP_SOURCE_LIGHT
- ARCHIVE_BINARY
- PURGE_BINARY_CANDIDATE
- HUMAN_REVIEW

These are separate from §11 row-retention classes. A source row and its attached bytes can therefore have different retention decisions.

### 13.5 Minimum durable media tombstone/fingerprint
Before KEEP_SOURCE_LIGHT or purge of original bytes, an existing owner must retain enough durable provenance to prove the media existed and explain what happened: source occurrence identity; original/source locator where meaningful; stable media/object identity when present; hash/fingerprint where available; MIME; byte size; original/derivative relation; surviving thumbnail/OCR/transcript references where applicable; disposition reason; executor/decision provenance and time. If existing carriers cannot preserve this without a broken or misleading live reference, purge fails closed to ARCHIVE_BINARY / HUMAN_REVIEW. No new Media/Retention Store is authorized.

### 13.6 Live-reader dependency
Media used by an active Broadcast/Contributor/Gallery/Post/Topic/World surface, featured-media projection, OCR/transcription process or another live reader is not purge-safe merely because extraction finished. Runtime readers are dependencies.

### 13.7 Physical dedup only
Strong media identity may deduplicate physical bytes; it never merges source occurrences. Each occurrence retains attribution/context/provenance. Filename, OCR equality or visual similarity alone cannot prove identity.

### 13.8 Derivatives
Regenerable thumbnails/transcodes/previews may receive shorter bounded retention after their owner proves lineage and regeneration. Original and derivative permissions remain separate.

### 13.9 Control Plane projection
The existing admin_retention_preview() / admin_system_health() boundary should expose, without a new store: original/derivative bytes; KEEP_ORIGINAL/KEEP_SOURCE_LIGHT/ARCHIVE/PURGE_CANDIDATE/HUMAN_REVIEW counts and bytes; research/load-bearing and active-surface blockers; orphan/duplicate candidates; largest/oldest/newest; projected reclaimable bytes; unknown dependency bytes; and Explain-Why/blocking references. Unknown is never counted as reclaimable savings.

### 13.10 Purge executor acceptance
PREVIEW / DRY RUN -> separate Human-Gated PURGE. Any future binary executor re-checks dependencies immediately before mutation, is idempotent/auditable/privacy-safe/recoverable, preserves source/tombstone provenance and must not silently break live URLs. Source-row deletion and binary deletion are separate decisions.

### 13.11 No guessed duration
No 30/90/180-day cleanup window is established here. Duration is a later per-class operational decision based on measured cost/growth, privacy/legal/audit, replay and reader dependencies.

### 13.12 Storage tiering
Storage provider/location is implementation, not semantic identity. Moving protected media to a cheaper archive may be valid if addressability, provenance, access and replay survive.

### 13.13 Governed self-maintenance
system_suggestions_law may observe storage growth/large objects/egress/missing derivatives/orphan candidates and recommend maintenance with evidence and estimated savings. It never self-applies destructive data changes. Production purge remains independently Human-Gated/release-gated.

### 13.14 Calibration only
On 20.9.2026, live channel_updates had 2,111 rows, 457 with media; 448 matched Storage objects consumed ≈1,673.68 MB, largest ≈42.91 MB. Calibration is not a purge decision.

**Result:** source fidelity and storage cost are both preserved. Provenance can survive without an unlimited binary-retention promise, while load-bearing media remains protected. No new retention/media system is created.

## §14 — OPEN GUIDED DEEPENING / DYNAMIC RESEARCH BRANCHES
Human-Gate ZURIEL · 2026-09-23 · OWNER CHECK=EXTEND_EXISTING

This section extends the existing Research Plan / Research Workspace / Experience projection. It creates no new Search Engine, Branch Store, Research OS, taxonomy owner or autonomous agent.

### 14.1 NOT A CLOSED BRANCH ENUM
The interface must not hard-code the universe of future research moves. Today we can name examples such as Source Context, Source-First Semantic Expansion, Cross-Method Comparison, Numeric Relation, Corpus Control, Contradiction Check, Alternate Representation, ELS, Sequence Research, Challenge and Explain-Why. These are examples, not a closed list.

A future registered capability may become available without redesigning the research screen. The Research Planner resolves current capabilities/owners live and may propose a branch whenever it can materially reduce uncertainty, add independent evidence, explain a contradiction, expose provenance, or open a relevant source-defined path.

### 14.2 RESULT -> NEW QUESTION -> OPTIONAL BRANCH
Every meaningful Finding/Synthesis may produce zero or more proposed next branches. A proposed branch should explain:
- what opened this branch;
- why it is relevant now;
- source/provenance/anchor that justified it;
- expected evidence class / information gain;
- estimated depth/cost/latency class where known;
- whether it is deterministic, source retrieval, AI-assisted, challenge, or another registered capability;
- stop/completion condition;
- what would count as a useful or negative result.

No branch is evidence merely because the Planner proposed it.

### 14.3 SOURCE-FIRST EXPANSION
When a source/source-locus itself supplies semantically related terms, phrases, persons, places, concepts or adjacent passages, the Planner may propose those as a bounded semantic expansion before looking at their numeric values.

Preferred sequence:
Exact Subject -> Exact Source/Locus -> Source-attested semantic family -> governed calculation/search -> normalized Cross -> Synthesis.

This prevents target-fitting such as choosing a phrase only because it hits a desired number. Search normalization does not change calculation identity; every source phrase/orthographic representation is calculated independently.

### 14.4 USER-VISIBLE "WHAT OPENED FROM THIS?"
The research UI may project a bounded set of branch cards under a result/Synthesis, for example:
- open the source context;
- deepen this number;
- follow a semantic family;
- compare another representation;
- challenge this interpretation;
- inspect why this connection exists;
- run a newly available registered capability.

Each card is a projection of the underlying Research Plan/Capability identity. The UI does not own branch semantics.

### 14.5 RECURSIVE BUT BOUNDED
A chosen branch may itself generate new branches. Recursion is allowed only while bounded by explicit budget/depth/completion rules. Unlimited mining is forbidden.

The user can move:
answer -> branch -> deeper branch -> challenge/alternative -> synthesis,
while Journey/Research Context preserves why/how the path was traversed.

### 14.6 THE SYSTEM MAY TEACH THE USER NEW RESEARCH MOVES
The Planner/Raziel may explain a newly relevant action in natural language:
- "מצאתי מקור שמרחיב את הביטוי; רוצה לבדוק את המילים שהמקור עצמו נותן?"
- "יש כאן שתי שיטות שתלויות זו בזו; רוצה שאחפש ראיות עצמאיות?"
- "נפתח כלי חדש שמתאים לשאלה הזאת; רוצה להפעיל אותו?"

This is guided research literacy, not an autonomous truth claim. New tools appear because they are registered under existing governance, not because AI invented a hidden capability.

### 14.7 LEARNING: RANK BETTER, DO NOT SELF-REWRITE TRUTH
System Suggestions / Champion-Challenger may learn which proposed branches tend to produce:
- decision-changing new Findings;
- independent evidence rather than duplicate derivations;
- useful contradiction/negative results;
- lower cost/latency for equivalent research value;
- higher user continuation/clarity (Resonance only, not truth).

Learning may change branch recommendation/ranking policy only through the existing governed Human-Gate path. It may not:
- create or authorize an engine/owner;
- alter canonical method semantics;
- infer that a branch is true because users like it;
- canonicalize/publish;
- silently widen privacy/access;
- turn frequency of selection into Research Strength.

### 14.8 OPEN QUESTIONS ARE RESUMABLE
A high-value branch that is not executed now may remain an existing Research Question/Open State under current Research OS semantics. It can be resumed later when budget, access, source material or a new capability becomes available.

### 14.9 FUTURE-PROOF ACTION PROJECTION
The renderer should consume a generic governed action/branch projection rather than one UI component per engine. Logical fields may include:
branch_id / capability / action_label / why_now / source_anchor / expected_information_gain / depth_class / estimated_cost_class / access_state / evidence_target / completion_condition / provenance.

This is a logical projection contract, not a schema mandate.

### 14.10 SAME TRUTH AT EVERY DEPTH
Going deeper may reveal more context, methods, sources, controls and alternative readings. It must not create a second truth system. Anonymous/Free/Premium differences may affect available volume, continuity, memory, tools and sustained depth, while correctness/verification standards remain governed by the same owners.

### 14.11 CALIBRATION EXAMPLE — NAME -> NUMBERS 3:35 -> ORAITA
The 2026-09-23 ZURIEL research session is the first worked UX calibration:
צוריאל פולייס -> Numbers 3:35 -> צוריאל בן אביחיל / מררי / מחלי ומושי -> 450 / 1237 -> אוריתא/אורייתא -> source-attested family (אורייתא קדישא / חד שמא קדישא / סתים וגליא / ישראל / אתברי עלמא) -> normalized Cross.

The decision-changing lesson is procedural, not personal truth:
the source selected the next semantic terms before their numbers were tested.
The UI should be able to expose that path and let the user choose which branch to deepen.

### 14.12 IMPLEMENTATION STATUS
DOCUMENTED BEHAVIOR / EXPERIENCE DIRECTION ONLY. No runtime Guided Deepening UI, branch executor or adaptive branch-ranking implementation is authorized by this section. Future implementation must extend existing Research Plan, Research Context/Journey, capability registry/adapters, System Suggestions learning and Experience projection. No parallel Search/Research system.

## §15 — G3 OPERATIONAL RETENTION CALIBRATION
**Human-Gate ZURIEL · 24.9.2026 · EXTEND_EXISTING §11/§13**

This section calibrates existing retention classes with current live decisions. It does not create a new retention system and does not override provenance/privacy/domain owners.

### 15.1 KEEP FOREVER / HISTORY AUTHORITY
- work_log;
- decision_ledger;
- Human-Gate provenance;
- canonical research/source decision history required for replay/provenance.
Age alone is never a deletion criterion.

### 15.2 KEEP LONG-TERM
- visitor_events;
- site_visits;
- traffic/analytics history including historical event partitions and GSC metrics;
unless a later Human-Gate decision defines aggregation+archive that preserves required historical analytics. Age alone is not purge authority.

### 15.3 BOUNDED RUNTIME
Operational telemetry may use bounded retention only under its existing owner:
- cron.job_run_details: current live retention = 14 days;
- delivery/debug/queue/token-analysis logs: candidate for bounded retention after reader/audit/provenance proof;
- wa_msg_ext and similar dedup/runtime metadata remain BOUNDED_RUNTIME candidates under §11.
Changing the duration requires measured need and owner verification, not guesswork.

### 15.4 ONE-OFF AUDIT / SNAPSHOT ARTIFACTS
One-off audit tables are temporary operational evidence, not permanent runtime authorities.
Lifecycle:
ACTIVE_AUDIT -> PURPOSE_COMPLETE -> PROVENANCE_SNAPSHOT -> DEPENDENCY_SCAN -> ARCHIVE_THEN_DROP.
A completed audit table with no live reader should not remain in the active DB merely because it may be historically interesting; durable provenance belongs in Git/work_log/approved archive evidence as appropriate.

Calibration example executed 24.9.2026:
public._audit_wp_thumb_candidates_20260828 was dependency-scanned, final counts preserved in work_log, Human-Gate drop authorized, then dropped; DB size fell by ~56 MiB. This is evidence for the lifecycle, not blanket permission to drop other audit tables.

### 15.5 CADENCE AS RETENTION/COST CONTROL
Polling and recovery cadence are part of operational cost control.
- Live source capture may remain frequent where user-visible freshness requires it.
- Heavy AI/research processing may run less frequently in batches if source capture remains lossless.
- Recovery jobs should not run at high frequency by default when the primary event-driven path is healthy; cadence must reflect recovery SLA, not habit.
Any cadence reduction must preserve backlog recovery, idempotency and freshness requirements.

### 15.6 Cleanup order
PREVIEW -> dependency proof -> provenance preservation -> Human Gate when destructive -> mutation -> live verification -> measured before/after capacity.
Unknown dependency remains HUMAN_REVIEW.
