## 1. North star

SOD1820 must be able to receive a Name, Number, Person, Date, Expression or another qualified research subject and produce a compact, explainable message without turning:
- raw match count into truth;
- method count into evidence count;
- AI eloquence into accuracy;
- user likes into verification;
- Tarot/randomness into evidence;
- personal context into public truth;
- one successful example into a fixed semantic law.

The system should improve over time by testing frozen claims against genuinely unseen/held-out material, cohort controls and contradictions.

The target is not a numerology answer engine.

The target is a **replayable symbolic-research laboratory** that can learn which patterns are useful, which are generic, which are mechanically dependent, which survive controls, and when the honest output is “insufficiently specific”.

## 3. Three things that must remain separate

### 3.1 Research Strength
How well a research structure stands:
- evidence independence;
- exact engine verification;
- source/provenance quality;
- specificity/base rate;
- cross-domain coherence;
- contradiction/control state;
- replayability;
- Human curation.

It is a **profile**, not one universal truth score.

### 3.2 Empirical Person Fit
How well a previously frozen set of atomic claims matches held-out information about the Person.

It is a **calibration measurement**, not verification, destiny, canonical truth or diagnosis.

A percentage may be shown only when the measurement protocol and sample are explicit.

### 3.3 Resonance / Engagement
Likes, dislikes, shares, “continue”, “research”, dwell/interaction and historical AI preference.

This tells us what users found compelling/useful.

It must not be silently converted into Research Strength or Person Fit.

---

## 4. Message Freeze — non-negotiable

A personal synthesis intended for accuracy measurement is frozen **before** validation data is revealed.

Freeze preserves:
- synthesis policy version;
- exact atomic claims;
- motifs;
- supporting Finding IDs;
- dependency groups;
- negative/control inputs;
- corpus/control version;
- model/provider used for wording;
- generated time;
- source Result Bundle contract/version.

After freeze, validation may only append outcomes. It may not rewrite the original claim to fit the person.

If the text changes materially, it becomes a new synthesis version and requires a new validation lineage.

---

## 5. Atomic claims before prose

The generated message is not the measurement unit.

Example prose:

> “You tend to turn connection into structure, and the challenge is amplifying the right thing.”

It must be decomposed into separately testable claims, e.g.:
- C1: recurrent integration/bridging tendency;
- C2: recurrent structure-building tendency;
- C3: amplification/intensification is a recurring pattern;
- C4: regulation/selection moderates the pattern.

Each claim preserves:
- role: interpretation/hypothesis/summary;
- motif key;
- support Finding IDs;
- dependency groups;
- derivations;
- controls/contradictions;
- validation state.

This lets the system learn that C1 may calibrate well while C4 does not.

## 6. Cross Signature — first-class synthesis fuel

Cross-method relation is not a new Synthesis calculation.

Canonical owner remains:
- `public.fn_relation_candidate(a,b)`;
- `public.cross_method_strength`;
- method dependency/equivalence registries;
- Research Strength / Convergence owners.

The Research capability layer must adapt governed Cross results into the Result Bundle before Synthesis consumes them.

A Cross Signature should preserve at minimum:
- exact endpoint identities / representations;
- relation source/version;
- matched values;
- raw method matches;
- effective independent method/dependency groups;
- derived/equivalent groups;
- position/word-order sensitivity where relevant;
- corpus/base-rate or rarity context when available;
- independent nonnumeric evidence refs;
- negative/noise flags;
- search-space/selection provenance;
- candidate status / no auto graph promotion.

**Rule:** “3 methods” is never displayed as 3 independent axes until dependency normalization proves it.

A multi-method Cross may become high-information Synthesis fuel when:
1. independent groups survive normalization;
2. the relation is specific enough under corpus controls;
3. the search space is disclosed;
4. no contradiction/control materially kills it;
5. semantic interpretation remains distinct from the numeric relation fact.

## 7. Method semantics are lenses, not fixed truth weights

Approved method semantics may inform the Field Map:
- `רגיל` → revealed/base identity;
- `מסתתר` → hidden layer;
- `מילוי` → inner/full-name layer;
- `סידורי` → order/position;
- `קדמי/משולש` → progression;
- `אותיות לפני` → step-back / prior form;
- `אותיות אחרי` → step-forward;
- `אלב״ם` → complement;
- `אתב״ש` → mirror;
- future methods only through their owner.

But no rule such as “hidden ×1.5” may be hard-coded merely because a method sounds deeper.

Learning may later discover context-specific predictive/calibration value, but that value is empirical and versioned, never retroactively redefines the mathematical method.

## 8. Corpus program

Corpus expansion is required, but larger corpus means larger multiple-comparison risk.

### 8.1 Preserve exact Expression identities
Never merge spelling/orthography merely because meanings are similar.

### 8.2 Add typed semantic relations instead of flat word lists
Useful relation families:
- root ↔ derivative;
- synonym;
- antonym;
- contrast/opposition;
- cause/effect candidate;
- whole/part;
- agent/action;
- place/person;
- biblical/source-attested;
- modern usage;
- traditional term;
- curated research expression;
- generated/synthetic phrase.

A semantic relation is provenance-bearing and does not alter Gematria calculation identity.

### 8.3 Corpus provenance
Every corpus term/relation should be able to answer:
- where it came from;
- exact wording;
- source/edition/locator where applicable;
- human/engine/generated origin;
- admission/review state;
- date/version;
- privacy/access class.

### 8.4 Base-rate recalibration
Whenever corpus size/composition materially changes, recompute:
- value frequencies;
- method-value frequencies;
- Cross frequencies;
- relation-family baselines;
- decoy/control distributions.

A finding must not keep an old rarity label after the search space changed.

### 8.5 Do not grow the corpus just to make a favored match appear
Expansion is source/coverage-driven, not result-driven.


### 8.6 Live corpus owner map — 2026-09-23
Live audit on canonical Supabase `linswmnnkjxvweumprav`:
- `gematria_words`: 15,521 rows; 12,642 engine-verified; 14,002 Hebrew-like; 2,000 tagged; 505 graph-linked.

- `words`: 12 rows; `word_tags`: 0; these are not a mature semantic owner and must not be inflated into a parallel ontology.
- `word_aliases`: 7 rows — identity/variant support only.
- `relation_evidence`: 132 rows — evidence about relations, not a general semantic dictionary.
- `word_review_queue`: 14 rows — admission/review workflow, not corpus truth by itself.

Canonical routing:
- Gematria expression admission stays under `corpus_admission_foundation_v1` / `corpus_admission_lifecycle_law` + Gematria owners.
- Durable semantic identity/relation belongs to Reality Graph / Research OS with typed provenance.
- Source-derived lexical coverage may use existing Intake/Source owners.
- No `semantic_dictionary` / synonym store / antonym store is authorized by this program.

Future synonym/antonym/root expansion therefore begins as source-attested Research/Graph relations or admission candidates and only persists under the existing owner that matches the semantic role.

## 9. Personal validation protocol

Preferred validation sources, from stronger to weaker:
1. held-out structured self-report collected after freeze;
2. defined/validated measurement;
3. directly reported biographical/life-event facts with provenance;
4. longitudinal future facts/events recorded after freeze;
5. independent familiar-person assessment where consent/scope allows;
6. free-form user impression.

Do not infer sensitive/protected traits from symbolic data.

### Claim outcomes
- supported;
- partially_supported;
- contradicted;
- not_testable;
- too_generic;
- untested.

### Required controls
- validation data hidden during synthesis;
- message freeze;
- decoy/shuffled messages;
- evaluator blindness where practical;
- leakage check;
- post-hoc exposure declaration;
- missingness;
- contradiction reporting.

## 10. Decoy / Barnum control

A message that “feels true” for everyone is not specific.

Tests may compare:
- correct frozen synthesis;
- syntheses from other Persons;
- motif-shuffled synthesis;
- generic high-Barnum baseline;
- random or same-demographic decoys only when privacy and study design justify it.

Metrics should include:
- correct-vs-decoy preference;
- rank of the correct message;
- lift over baseline;
- genericity penalty;
- contradiction rate.

No single metric becomes Truth.

## 11. Longitudinal validation

The most valuable validation is evidence that did not exist at synthesis time.

For future checks preserve:
- prediction/claim freeze time;
- allowed future horizon;
- event/fact definition;
- source;
- whether the outcome is genuinely new;
- whether an evaluator knew the original message.

Do not rewrite vague claims after the event.

## 13. Multiple AI providers

Claude, Gemini and GPT are **providers/models**, not three research truths.

Canonical flow:
```text
one Result Bundle / one Synthesis context
        ↓
provider/model chosen by plan, cost, depth or challenge need
        ↓
renderer / challenger / evaluator
        ↓
one governed Synthesis lineage
```

### Normal mode
Use one suitable model.

### Challenge mode
A second model may independently:
- challenge unsupported claims;
- detect alternative motifs;
- flag over-reading;
- compress or improve wording.

### Ensemble/robustness mode
For high-impact/private/premium research, models may independently synthesize from the same frozen evidence pack.

Agreement is a **model robustness signal**, not independent evidence about the Person.

Disagreement is valuable and must remain visible to the planner.

Never use majority vote to manufacture truth.

## 14. Tarot / random symbolic probes

Tarot remains an optional **Auxiliary Signal** only.

It may never:
- increase empirical Person Fit;
- increase Research Strength;
- verify a claim;
- change governance/canonicality;
- alter the frozen message after seeing the cards.

Research protocol:
1. freeze synthesis;
2. draw cards;
3. preserve exact cards/orientations;
4. evaluate resonance using a fixed rubric;
5. compare with random-control draws;
6. analyze only at cohort scale whether real draws outperform controls.

Current `fn_tarot_sos` uses nondeterministic `random()`; future research-grade use needs a replayable draw identity/seed or a persisted exact draw record before claiming reproducibility.

Until that exists, exact returned cards may be stored as observed auxiliary output, but the RNG operation itself is not replayable.

## 15. Learning loop — Champion / Challenger

The system may learn, but it may not silently rewrite itself.

### Champion
Current active synthesis/calibration policy.

### Challenger
Candidate policy derived from:
- accumulated calibrated claims;
- cohort patterns;
- new corpus baselines;
- model disagreement;
- failures/contradictions;
- historical analysis replay.

### Evaluation
Run Champion and Challenger on frozen historical cases and fresh holdout cases.

Measure separately:
- Person-fit calibration;
- specificity/decoy lift;
- contradiction rate;
- coverage;
- latency/cost;
- provider dependence;
- fairness/privacy leakage;
- stability across corpus versions.

### Promotion
A Challenger may be proposed through existing `learned_patterns` / Research OS / decision path.

It never auto-promotes itself.

Human Gate decides whether a synthesis policy changes.

## 16. Accuracy language

Avoid:
- “the system knows you with 91% accuracy”;
- “91% true”;
- “Tarot confirmed the reading”.

Allowed when protocol supports it:
- “Empirical fit on 8 testable frozen claims: 75% supported/partially supported; 12.5% contradicted; 12.5% unresolved.”
- “Cohort holdout lift over decoy baseline: +18 points across N independent Persons.”
- “Historical resonance: high; research strength: moderate.”
- “Not yet calibrated.”

Always preserve sample size and protocol.

## 17. Performance / cost

Default order:
1. use compiled/cached deterministic facts keyed by input + owner/method/version;
2. dependency-normalize before expensive expansion;
3. shortlist high-information Cross candidates;
4. run corpus controls only for candidates that can change synthesis;
5. use one AI model for ordinary rendering;
6. use challenger models only when Information Gain justifies cost;
7. deep/Premium expands the same object instead of recomputing a parallel answer.

Compile-on-change, not on every page request.

Invalidation dimensions must include:
- corpus version;
- method/engine version;
- relation/dependency version;
- synthesis policy version;
- source revision where applicable.

## 19. Privacy and Person learning

Personal Foundation remains authoritative.

Required separation:
`STORE != RETRIEVE != PARTICIPATE != ANALYZE != PROMOTE != CANONICALIZE != PUBLISH`.

Personal evidence stays private by default.

Cohort learning:
- must distinguish within-person recurrence from cross-person recurrence;
- must avoid small-group/re-identification leakage;
- uses privacy-safe aggregated features;
- never publishes another Person’s private evidence in Explain-Why;
- never infers protected/sensitive traits from Name/Date patterns.

A Person does not inherit a trait because a Number has a candidate semantic profile.

## 20. Negative evidence

Synthesis must consume, not hide:
- NEGATIVE_RESULT;
- EXECUTED_EMPTY;
- MISSING_ADAPTER;
- UNVERIFIED;
- CONTEXT_REQUIRED;
- contradictions;
- failed Cross controls;
- genericity;
- insufficient sample;
- partial corpus coverage.

A negative result can lower or change the message.

## 22. Acceptance gates

Foundation is not closed until tests prove:
1. Synthesis receives only access-filtered Bundle material.
2. It cannot cite Findings absent from the Bundle.
3. Derived/equivalent evidence cannot inflate independent support.
4. Empirical fit cannot exist without a prior message freeze.
5. Decoy lift cannot exist without a decoy-control declaration.
6. Tarot/auxiliary output has zero empirical evidence weight.
7. Universal truth/accuracy score fields fail closed.
8. Synthesis failure does not destroy the underlying Result Bundle.
9. Cross Signature is owner-native/adapted, not reimplemented.
10. Personal/private inputs do not leak through synthesis/provenance.
11. Historical engagement never becomes verification.
12. A new AI provider joins as renderer/challenger without creating a new truth contract.
13. Old Number/AI consumers can migrate to the same synthesis without retaining local semantic authority.
14. Expensive work is bounded/versioned/replayable.
