## 1. Brand identity

Public Hebrew identity: **כי לה׳ המלוכה**.
Public English brand: **KINGDOM RISE**.
Canonical English line: **For the Kingdom Is the Lord’s**.
Technical / international system identity: **SOD1820**.

Hebrew and English are language projections of one brand. They use the **same Master Crown**; there is no separate English logo, symbol, crown lineage or visual identity.

Brand story:

**כ־15 שנות דרך · עידן חדש**

Supporting line:

**אותו שורש. התגלמות חדשה.**

The transition is a brand evolution, not a deletion of history.

## 2. Heritage Mark

The existing crown at `/logo.png` is preserved as the official **Heritage Mark**.

It represents continuity, recognition, archive provenance and the visual memory of the long-running site/video identity.

Rules:

- do not delete or silently replace the Heritage Mark;
- it may appear in archive/history/About/brand-story contexts and in a bounded Heritage seal/lockup;
- old videos and historical material are not considered visually obsolete merely because they carry the Heritage Mark;
- historical use is part of the brand story, not a migration defect.

## 3. Primary Digital Mark

Human Gate approved the **blue-gold crown** as the direction for the future **Primary Digital Mark**.

The exact master asset is still pending final production approval/export. Until that asset exists and is separately released, the product must not fake a final path or silently repoint `LOGO_URL`.

The Primary Digital Mark must preserve recognizable crown lineage while gaining:

- blue-gold digital material language;
- clean small-size silhouette;
- dark/light-safe projection;
- motion readiness;
- 3D/spatial readiness;
- favicon/app-icon/compact-lockup variants;
- reduced-motion/static fallback.

The same approved Primary Crown is used for both language lockups:

- Hebrew: **כי לה׳ המלוכה**
- English: **KINGDOM RISE**
- English explanatory/canonical line: **For the Kingdom Is the Lord’s**

The English wording does not create a second brand identity. `KINGDOM RISE` is the concise English brand name; `For the Kingdom Is the Lord’s` preserves the intended canonical meaning.

## 4. Hierarchy

There is one brand identity with multiple governed representations.

- Primary Digital Mark = future default product mark.
- Heritage Mark = preserved historical/continuity mark.
- Heritage Lockup = bounded composition showing both, with Primary dominant.
- Compact mark = simplified small-size projection of the same Primary identity.
- Motion / 3D = richer representation of the same identity, not a new logo.
- Hebrew / English = language lockups of the same Master Mark, never competing crowns.

Do not present Heritage and Primary as two equal competing main logos.

## 5. Transition / comeback behavior

Approved transition concept:

Heritage crown → controlled light/depth transformation → Primary blue-gold crown.

Purpose: communicate continuity and comeback.

Allowed contexts:

- first-visit/home hero moments;
- About / brand-story experience;
- launch/comeback campaign;
- new-video intro/outro;
- special share/announcement cards.

Not allowed:

- random logo swapping every few seconds;
- changing brand mark without context on ordinary navigation;
- autoplay motion that cannot degrade under `prefers-reduced-motion`;
- motion or 3D that changes brand identity/truth semantics.

Recommended public copy family:

- `כ־15 שנות דרך · עידן חדש`
- `אותו שורש. התגלמות חדשה.`
- `אותה הדרך — עולם חדש.`
- `KINGDOM RISE`
- `For the Kingdom Is the Lord’s`

## 6. 3D / animation continuity

This Brand Evolution consumes the existing `spatial_gematria_law` progressive representation principle:

2D mark → motion/depth projection → richer 3D representation only when useful.

Same identity must persist across tiers. 3D is a presentation tier, not a stronger truth/importance state.

The target 3D crown may use real material depth, gemstone illumination and controlled camera movement, but a static canonical fallback must always exist.

The same 3D crown must support Hebrew and English lockups without re-modeling a second crown.

## 7. Public surfaces

About and Contact are explicit brand-story surfaces and should explain the continuity rather than presenting the new identity as an unexplained replacement.

Future redesigned surfaces must consume the shared brand identity projection rather than writing local brand-history copy.

The current sitewide `/logo.png` usage remains Heritage-compatible until the final Primary asset is approved and a separate release explicitly promotes it.

## 8. Heritage / Primary usage policy

The old and new crowns are not two competing styles. They encode **time/context** within one brand lineage.

### 8.1 Preserve context, do not repaint history

- Historical posts, videos, galleries and campaign assets may retain the Heritage Crown when that mark reflects the era in which the material was created or first published.
- Do not mass-replace the Heritage Crown on old media merely for visual uniformity.
- The Heritage Crown should remain available in the same canonical brand-master context so future redesign, archival display and provenance can always reconstruct the visual lineage.
- Historical presence of the old crown is a feature of provenance and continuity, not design debt.

### 8.2 New-era default

After the explicit brand cutover, newly created public brand-bearing assets default to the Primary blue-gold crown.

This includes, once the final Master Asset is released:

- new post/share-card branding;
- new video intros/outros and watermarks;
- new campaign/announcement graphics;
- redesigned public surfaces;
- new app/icon projections;
- new motion/3D representations.

### 8.3 Strong Hints / flagship editorial example

`רמזים חזקים` is the flagship editorial family and may expose the lineage more visibly than ordinary content.

Recommended behavior:

- older flagship posts may retain the Heritage Crown;
- new flagship posts use the Primary Crown plus the new Strong Hints / Diamond Signature when applicable;
- the crown communicates brand era/lineage; the Diamond Signature communicates the editorial family. They must not collapse into one semantic mark;
- a historical post that is merely resurfaced does **not** automatically become visually “new”.

### 8.4 Re-editions / major remasters

When an old post is materially rebuilt for the new system, choose explicitly between:

1. **Historical continuity** — keep Heritage Crown and add a bounded note such as `עודכן במערכת החדשה` / `Remastered in the new system`; or
2. **New edition** — preserve the original historical item/provenance and create a clearly new edition/projection using the Primary Crown.

Do not silently overwrite the old era marker when the historical identity matters.

### 8.5 Archive and discovery surfaces

Archive/search/category surfaces may use small, non-intrusive lineage labels only when they genuinely help orientation, for example:

- `מורשת`
- `הכתר המקורי`
- `הדור החדש`

Do not place these labels on every card by default. The archive should remain readable; lineage is contextual metadata, not a permanent badge tax.

### 8.6 Where the transition is explained

Explain the brand transition in a small number of deliberate places:

- About / brand story;
- Contact, in compact form;
- a dedicated `כ־15 שנות דרך · עידן חדש` story/launch surface when built;
- optional first-visit/home comeback moment;
- special launch/share/video contexts.

Do **not** repeat the explanation inside every post or image.

### 8.7 Cutover rule

A single explicit **Brand Cutover Date** must be recorded when the final Primary Master Crown asset is released.

Default interpretation after that release:

- content/assets first published before the cutover → Heritage-eligible;
- content/assets first published after the cutover → Primary by default;
- exceptions require an explicit editorial/heritage reason, not arbitrary styling.

Until the final Master Crown asset is released, no cutover date is considered active.

### 8.8 Pinned / selected / editorial promotion is a separate axis

Brand era is independent from editorial promotion.

`Heritage / Primary` must not be inferred from:

- pinned status;
- 14-day promotion;
- `נבחרים שלנו`;
- Strong Hints membership;
- truth/verification/canonicality;
- popularity or traffic.

A post may be Heritage + pinned, Primary + pinned, Heritage + selected, or Primary + selected. Brand lineage and editorial promotion remain separate semantics.

### 8.9 Governing principle

**העבר נשמר כהקשר. העתיד מוצג כהתגלמות החדשה.**

Preserve provenance first; project the new brand forward rather than repainting the past.

## 9. Release boundary

This direction does not itself authorize:

- replacing `/logo.png` globally;
- deleting old logo assets;
- changing video watermarks in historical media;
- mass regenerating legacy media;
- deploying a new animated/3D logo runtime;
- merging or deploying this branch.

Those require the normal Human Gate/release path.
