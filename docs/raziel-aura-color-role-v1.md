# SOD1820 — Raziel Aura Color Role V1

Status: HUMAN-GATE LOCKED DESIGN PROJECTION · BRANCH ONLY · NOT MERGED · NOT DEPLOYED

Owner: `SOD1820_DESIGN_CONTRACT_V1.md`

OWNER CHECK: `EXTEND_EXISTING`.

This memo refines the already-locked Horizon + Cosmic UI System 2029 without creating a new palette or control system.

## Locked semantic color hierarchy

- **Gold** = Master Brand / Premium / ceremonial value / major orientation emphasis.
- **Sapphire / Research Blue** = primary action / research interaction / functional digital state.
- **Purple / Indigo** = Raziel / intelligence / companion aura / advanced insight atmosphere.

Purple is not a generic UI accent and must not become a competing color family across all controls.

## Raziel color role

Raziel may use a controlled Indigo → Royal Purple treatment, staying visually adjacent to the Crown sapphire family while remaining clearly identifiable as the Companion/Intelligence layer.

Recommended material targets:

- Raziel Indigo: `#5B3FD6`
- Raziel Royal Purple: `#7A4CE0`
- Raziel halo: `rgba(122,76,224,0.28)`
- optional Celestial edge light: `#6CB6FF`
- fixed gold core/detail: `#D4AF37`

The aura may move or breathe slowly between Blue → Indigo → Royal Purple → Blue when motion is appropriate. The Crown itself remains stable Gold + Sapphire; only environment/aura motion may shift.

## Control usage

- Premium / Upgrade / Open Gate remains Gold/Champagne, not Purple.
- Primary Explore/Open/Continue remains Sapphire/Deep Blue.
- Share remains neutral Glass; hover/focus may carry a restrained Blue–Purple aura.
- Save remains neutral Glass; saved state keeps clear semantic state and may use a restrained Horizon ring, not a full Purple fill.
- Follow/Watch may lean slightly more into Purple/Orbit treatment when active because its semantic metaphor is orbital/ongoing attention.
- Add-to-Research remains Research Blue.
- Raziel-specific actions such as Ask Raziel / Analyze with Raziel / Open with Raziel may use the Indigo → Royal Purple family as their owned accent.
- Advanced AI/insight/spatial-companion states may use Purple only when they are genuinely Raziel/intelligence projections.

## Guardrails

- No rainbow treatment.
- No neon magenta/pink dominance.
- No fast color cycling.
- Do not recolor Crown gemstones or Master Crown identity dynamically.
- Purple must not override world/domain classification colors or status/truth semantics.
- Dark and Light remain the same product: dark uses richer Indigo/Purple aura; light uses restrained lavender/indigo edge/halo while preserving contrast and accessibility.
