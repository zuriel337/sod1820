## 1. Core decision

SOD1820 remains an open research system. New research laws, numerical relationships, pattern families, geometry criteria and date rules must be addable without dismantling the engine or creating a parallel system.

**New law = registered research rule/capability on the shared substrate; never a new engine, parallel tree or alternate source of truth.**

Lifecycle remains: `idea → candidate → reconstructed → verified → approved/canonical`, with provenance at every step. Candidate is never silently treated as fact.

## 2. Shared Research Time Layer

Time becomes a first-class research dimension over the shared Research Objects.

`Source → Term/Expression → Derived Date Candidate → Year/Date → Timeline Position → Event → Other Research Objects`

and in reverse:

`Known Event/Year → Timeline → Candidate Expressions → ELS / Gematria / Sources → Findings`

This is a view/capability, not a separate timeline engine.

A date-like finding must preserve separately:

- **FACT** — exact text/expression found.
- **DERIVED** — deterministic numerical/calendar interpretation.
- **INTERPRETATION** — historical/thematic reading.
- **CONFIDENCE** — ambiguity/strength of the date interpretation.
- **PROVENANCE** — source, extraction, rule/version, calculation and human decision.

A year-like expression does not automatically become a historical fact.

### Temporal Anchors

Existing strong, human-reviewed date-linked ciphers may become **Temporal Anchors**. They are stable research objects that allow later questions such as:

- What else occurs around this year?
- Do independent ciphers converge on the same period?
- Which sources/events connect to the same date?
- Are there unusual clusters around a period?

Density is a research signal, not proof.

### Timeline views

One shared timeline may support chronological, entity, research, cross-time and reverse-time views without creating separate systems.

## 3. Numerical scale / zero relationships

The research layer may investigate explicit scale transformations such as:

`358 ↔ 3580 ↔ 35800`

but these are **not numerical equality**. The relation is stored as an explicit transformation, e.g. `3580 ÷ 10 = 358`.

Separate:

`value · transform · derived_family · interpretation`

This extends existing zero/scale research without changing the underlying calculated value.

## 4. Leading-digit / "one" relationships

A transformation such as:

`1358 → remove-leading-1 → 358`

is an interpretive research transformation, not ordinary numerical equality. The transformation and the conclusion drawn from it remain separate.

This is deliberately represented as a registered transformation so future researcher-defined transformations do not become hard-coded special cases inside ELS or gematria.

## 5. Skip value as research dimension

ELS skip is a first-class finding attribute, not merely a display number. Preserve, where available:

`term · skip · direction · start/exact occurrence · geometry · window · proximity · gap · occurrence count · scope · ranking inputs · provenance`

Future research may test skip-value recurrence, skip-to-number relationships and cross-layer support. Skip value alone does not establish meaning. Statistical claims require an explicit null model and bounded experiment.

## 6. Geometry and structured-cipher preference

Geometry is a research capability distinct from visual Zoom/Focus.

A finding may carry axis orientation, matrix dimensions, line/shape structure, crossing density, symmetry/repetition, compactness, secondary axes and relative placement of related terms.

A future structured-form score may rank unusually organized geometry, but must not silently replace the current engine ranking.

**Interesting geometry is a reason to investigate, not a declaration of truth.**

Strongly structured findings may justify deeper spatial analysis, including 3D, while preserving the same finding identity and provenance.

## 7. Secondary axis → primary axis

The research loop supports:

`Finding → Promote to Main Axis → Research Around New Axis`

The previous axis remains preserved in Research Path/evidence history. Promotion is navigation/research, not deletion.

The chain may be long, but every transition is explicit and recorded. No uncontrolled automatic search expansion.

## 8. Extensible Research Law Registry

The system must accept new laws without structural surgery.

Future law families may include numerical transforms, skip-pattern families, date/calendar rules, geometry descriptors, source types, orthographic transformations, null-model experiments and research-quality factors.

A future law should declare at minimum:

`law_id · name · domain · input · deterministic rule · output · version · status · provenance · dependencies · cost · risk`

and, when it affects discovery/ranking:

`coverage · decision impact · null model / validation method`

Implementation never implies canonization.

## 9. Ranking without hidden bias

Future quality/ranking may combine:

`tightness + proximity + rarity + length + geometry + skip-pattern + cross-layer support + temporal support`

but each factor remains inspectable and measurable. No single factor may silently dominate.

Any quality-model change requires proposal → benchmark → saved-finding regression → impact analysis → Human-Gate.

## 10. AI Research Navigator

AI is a **Research Navigator**, not a source of truth. It may propose paths, rank next steps, estimate cost/value, challenge findings, propose null models, test parameter dependence, identify post-hoc selection, compare alternatives and summarize evidence.

AI must not calculate facts that belong to deterministic engines, silently expand search space, turn candidates into facts, promote canon, publish or override Human-Gate.

### AI Research Budget

Deep research is bounded by:

`estimated compute · search-space size · expected value · coverage · risk · decision impact`

Prefer the cheapest test that can change the decision.

### AI Challenge Mode

For important findings, challenge:

- alternative explanations
- null models
- parameter dependence
- post-hoc target selection
- pre-registered/bounded robustness
- independence of evidence

## 11. Shared views / identity preservation

2D, 3D, Source, Numerical, Graph, Timeline and Experiment are views/capabilities over the same Research Objects.

Preserve across views:

`finding_id · provenance · evidence · source · engine/version · parameters`

3D remains **Matrix State / Spatial Analysis**, never a second ELS engine.

## 12. Research Path / Timeline / Snapshot

A meaningful research session should eventually be reproducible through:

- **Research Path** — ordered actions/transitions.
- **Timeline** — temporal context of evidence/events.
- **Snapshot** — frozen research-object set, parameters and engine versions at a moment.

The system should answer: **How did we get from this input to this finding?** without relying on chat memory.

## 13. Whole-system integration

These capabilities belong to the same substrate as ELS, Gematria, Name/Entity Research, Tanakh sources, Reality Signals, news/event intake, Research Contributions, Research Workspace, AI/Raziel/Metatron, community research and 2D/3D/Graph/Experiment views.

No separate ELS timeline, ELS AI, ELS graph or ELS date engine.

Shared pipeline:

`Raw Input → Extraction → Calculation → Discovery → Evidence → AI/Challenge → Human Decision → Canonical Knowledge`

## 14. Decision gate

Every future capability is evaluated by:

`Object → Value → Cost → Coverage → Risk → Decision Impact`

and:

**Can the result change a decision?**

If not, do not spend research compute merely to obtain another number.

North Star:

**Maximum useful research / Minimum unnecessary computation.**

## 16. Epistemic rule

Always preserve:

**FOUND / CALCULATED / DERIVED / CANDIDATE / INTERPRETED / VERIFIED / CANONICAL / PUBLISHED**

An impressive ELS shape, unusual skip, date candidate, numerical transformation or AI explanation may be highly interesting while remaining a candidate. Preserve and rank it; do not silently upgrade its status.
