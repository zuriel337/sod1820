> Source artifact: *אהבת תורה*, ר' פנחס זלמן הלוי סג"ל איש־הורוויץ, Podgórze/Kraków, תרס"ה / 1905. HebrewBooks item #5635, 99-page scan. Research based on the primary scan used in the GPT session; Hebrew numeral/OCR readings remain provisional where the scan was not visually re-checked.

## 1. Research status

Current phase: **PRIMARY-SOURCE EXTRACTION / METHOD MAPPING**.

The objective is not to collect attractive 1820 correspondences. The current pass asks:

1. What populations does Horowitz define before counting?
2. What is counted, classified, ordered, bounded, normalized, included/excluded, or reconciled?
3. Which totals are decomposed enough to audit?
4. Which claims are direct corpus measurements, which are arithmetic/gematria constructions, and which are interpretation?
5. Which source claims can later be independently reconstructed from a canonical corpus/engine?

Truth separation used in this ledger:

- **SOURCE VERIFIED** — the primary scan supports that Horowitz makes the claim.
- **NEEDS CORPUS VERIFICATION** — a textual count/classification still needs independent reconstruction.
- **NEEDS ENGINE VERIFICATION** — a gematria/math claim must be checked through the live canonical engine/registry before being called verified.
- **INTERPRETATION** — Horowitz's or our interpretive layer, not a measured fact.
- **SCAN/OCR UNCERTAIN** — exact reading still needs visual page confirmation.

Additional provenance distinctions now required by the source:

- **AUTHOR_COUNT** — Horowitz explicitly marks a count as his own (for example, "לפי חשבוני").
- **CITED_COUNT** — Horowitz attributes a count/set to Masorah, חז"ל or another cited tradition/source.
- **COUNTING REGIME** — the inclusion/exclusion/read-vs-written rules under which a count is produced. Two counts can differ without one automatically invalidating the other.
- **RAW COUNT** — the direct count before any source-specific normalization or exclusion.
- **ADJUSTED COUNT** — the count after explicit textual/masoretic rules are applied.

## 2. Book map — current working view

### A. Quantitative / structural research layer

The first major layer contains the strongest material for method reconstruction: counts of Divine Names, word counts, speakers, repeated-word populations, "roots"/lexical units, and letter-by-parasha tables.

### B. Parasha-ordered chiddushim

A later layer is arranged by Torah portions and contains local interpretations, comparisons, arithmetic and textual observations. It should be mined after the quantitative layer is exhausted, with priority given only to genuinely new research methods or reproducible structural findings.

### C. Esther + addenda

The book also contains material on Esther and later additions/omissions. These remain lower priority until the quantitative layer is fully mapped.

## 3. Major source findings preserved so far

### 3.1 1,820 occurrences of the Tetragrammaton

**SOURCE VERIFIED. NEEDS CORPUS VERIFICATION.**

Horowitz states that he counted the Tetragrammaton from Genesis through the end of Torah and found **1,820** occurrences. He supplies parasha-level detail and book totals, explicitly so the reader can "stand on/check the count".

Book totals read from the primary scan:

- Genesis: **165**
- Exodus: **398**
- Leviticus: **311**
- Numbers: **396**
- Deuteronomy: **550**
- Total: **1,820**

Method significance: **TOTAL → PARASHA DECOMPOSITION → BOOK SUBTOTALS → TORAH TOTAL**. The decomposition functions as a manual audit trail.

### 3.2 1,830 Adonai counting category

**SOURCE VERIFIED. NEEDS CORPUS / MASORETIC VERIFICATION.**

Horowitz does not simply count written occurrences of אדנ"י. He distinguishes written form from reading identity: from 1,820 Tetragrammaton occurrences, he states that **1,816 are read as Adonai**, while four are read as Elohim, then adds **14 written sacred Adonai occurrences**, giving **1,830**.

Classification:

- 1,816 read-as-Adonai occurrences: **TEXT COUNT / READING-IDENTITY COUNT**.
- +14 written sacred Adonai occurrences: **TEXT COUNT**.
- 1,816 + 14 = 1,830: **CALCULATION**.

Method significance:

**WRITTEN FORM ≠ READ FORM ≠ COUNTING IDENTITY.**

This is a counting population created partly by reading tradition, not simple string matching.

### 3.3 Letter samekh: raw count 1,833 → normalized 1,830

**SOURCE VERIFIED. RULE-INDEPENDENCE NOT VERIFIED.**

Horowitz states that his letter count gives **1,833** occurrences of samekh, then discusses masoretic/textual exceptions including the tradition concerning the first samekh and the marks around ויהי בנסוע / inverted nuns, arriving at **1,830** and comparing that total to his Adonai count.

Classification:

- 1,833: **RAW TEXT COUNT / AUTHOR_COUNT**.
- exclusion of three under source-specific rules: **COUNTING REGIME / NORMALIZATION**.
- 1,833 − 3 = 1,830: **CALCULATION**.
- comparison to Adonai 1,830: **NUMERICAL CORRESPONDENCE / INTERPRETATION**.

Method significance:

**RAW COUNT → MASORETIC/TEXTUAL RULES → NORMALIZED COUNT → NUMERICAL CONVERGENCE.**

Critical open question: were the normalization rules independently justified before noticing the 1,830 convergence, or selected/adapted because of it? The source proves Horowitz's procedure, not its statistical or methodological independence.

### 3.4 יעקב + רחל + לאה = 1,820 spoken words

**SOURCE VERIFIED. NEEDS CORPUS VERIFICATION.**

Horowitz states that Jacob, Rachel and Leah together speak **1,820 words** in the Torah, "as the count of the Names of YHWH in the Torah". The primary scan gives:

- Jacob: **1,597**
- Rachel: **107**
- Leah: **116**
- Total: **1,820**

Jacob is further decomposed by parasha (including Toldot, Vayetze, Vayishlach, Vayeshev, Miketz, Vayigash, Vayechi).

Method significance: **CHARACTER / SPEAKER ATTRIBUTION → SPEECH SPANS → PARASHA SUBTOTALS → SPEAKER TOTAL → CROSS-DATASET CONVERGENCE.**

Open question: exact rules for quotation boundaries, repeated/report speech, joint speech, and attribution.

### 3.5 אוריין תליתאי — 79,976 Torah words divided into three speaker/source classes

**SOURCE VERIFIED. NEEDS CORPUS VERIFICATION.**

Horowitz states a total of **79,976 words** in the Torah and divides them into three broad classes:

- words of God: **26,155**
- words of the Torah/narrative: **27,261**
- words of the patriarchs, Moses and other people: **26,560**
- total: **79,976**

The section does not stop at three totals. It further decomposes at least part of the human-speech class into named speakers/groups, with counts of speeches and words.

The primary scan also contains a parasha-level word-count breakdown whose Torah-wide sum is **79,976**, giving a second audit route inside the same source.

Method significance: a manually attributed semantic corpus:

**TORAH → SOURCE/SPEAKER CLASS → NAMED SPEAKER/GROUP → SPEECH COUNT → WORD COUNT → SUBTOTALS → TOTAL.**

Open work: exhaust the section speaker-by-speaker and recover all boundary/attribution rules.

### 3.6 "שרשים בתורה" — 1,820 units according to Horowitz's own lexical scheme

**SOURCE VERIFIED. NEEDS NORMALIZATION + CORPUS VERIFICATION.**

Horowitz says, "לפי חשבוני מצאתי אלף תת"ך שרשים בתורה" and supplies a long list ordered by **first appearance in Torah**, not alphabetically.

This is explicitly an **AUTHOR_COUNT**.

Important terminology caution: do **not** silently equate all 1,820 items with modern grammatical triliteral roots. The list must first be reconstructed according to Horowitz's own identity/normalization scheme. Until that rule is understood, the safe label is:

**"שורשים/יחידות לקסיקליות בשיטת הורוויץ".**

Method significance:

**LEXICAL IDENTITY → FIRST-APPEARANCE ORDER → NOVELTY LIST → TOTAL 1,820.**

Open work: extract the full list and infer/verify his identity rule from edge cases.

### 3.7 "סופר ומונה אותיות התורה" — letter × parasha matrix and 304,812-letter total

**SOURCE STRUCTURE VERIFIED. TORAH TOTAL SOURCE VERIFIED. CELL VALUES NOT YET FULLY EXTRACTED. NEEDS CORPUS VERIFICATION.**

Immediately after the roots list, Horowitz begins "סופר ומונה אותיות התורה" and gives letter counts parasha by parasha and book subtotals, then continues through later letters.

The scan gives a Torah-wide total of **304,812 letters** for this counting system.

The structure is approximately:

**LETTER → PARASHA → OCCURRENCE COUNT → BOOK SUBTOTAL → TORAH TOTAL.**

This is a source count, not yet an independently reconstructed canonical-corpus fact. Token/text-version and orthographic rules still matter.

Open work: exhaust all letters א–ת, confirm page boundaries, subtotals and any methodological exceptions visually from the scan.

### 3.8 Repeated consecutive words / דרש דרש midpoint

**SOURCE VERIFIED. NEEDS FULL POPULATION RECONSTRUCTION.**

Horowitz builds a population of consecutive repeated words. His raw population places one repeated pair at the midpoint, creating tension with the traditional statement that **דרש דרש** is at the middle. He proposes excluding **לך לך** and **שם שם** because, in his terminology, they are not "from one root", after which **דרש דרש** becomes the midpoint of the normalized population.

The scan also identifies positional landmarks such as first/middle/last repeated forms; exact full population size was **not** securely read and must not be inferred from OCR.

Method significance:

**DETECT POPULATION → ORDER → FIND MIDPOINT → CONTRADICTION → REFINE ELIGIBILITY/SEMANTIC IDENTITY → RECOMPUTE.**

Critical question: whether the "same root" exclusion rule applies consistently across the full population or is post-hoc reconciliation.

### 3.9 Bounded-span word counts

**SOURCE VERIFIED IN PART. NEEDS FULL VISUAL EXTRACTION.**

Horowitz repeatedly defines internal textual units by explicit boundaries ("from ... to ..."), counts each span, then combines subtotals. One examined example culminates in **239 words**.

Method significance:

**START BOUNDARY → END BOUNDARY → SPAN COUNT → SUBTOTALS → COMBINED TOTAL.**

This is separate from parasha-wide counting and makes boundary definition itself part of the research method.

### 3.10 Cross-text numerical reconciliation

**SOURCE VERIFIED AS HOROWITZ'S INTERPRETIVE PROCEDURE.**

In places where parallel biblical passages give apparently different numerical reports, Horowitz explicitly says the verses need to be compared and proposes a numerical decomposition to reconcile them.

Method significance:

**SOURCE A → SOURCE B → APPARENT CONTRADICTION → DECOMPOSITION → PROPOSED RECONCILIATION.**

This must remain classified as **INTERPRETIVE RECONCILIATION**, not corpus fact.

### 3.11 Entity-identity reasoning (Serah example)

**SOURCE VERIFIED IN METHOD SHAPE; EXACT NUMERIC DETAIL STILL REQUIRES CLEAN VISUAL PASS.**

In a discussion of women named in the Torah, Horowitz treats repeated appearances of Serah as the same entity across contexts, invoking tradition about her longevity.

Method significance: **multiple textual mentions can resolve to one entity identity**.

This is complementary to the repeated-word case, where identical written tokens can be split into semantically different units.

### 3.12 Thirty two-letter words — explicit multi-path convergence around 1,830 and 2,501

**SOURCE STRUCTURE VERIFIED. ARITHMETIC PARTIALLY INDEPENDENTLY CHECKED. FULL WORD-SET + GEMATRIA ENGINE VERIFICATION STILL OPEN.**

Horowitz cites a Masoretic set of **30 words of two letters each** and performs several linked numerical constructions around it.

Source-derived structure:

- 30 words × 2 letters = **60 letters**.
- Horowitz connects 60 to samekh and to the arithmetic sum 1 through 60.
- 1 + 2 + ... + 60 = **1,830**.
- Horowitz states that the gematria total of the 30-word set is **2,501**.
- He gives the filling of אדנ"י as **671**.
- 1,830 + 671 = **2,501**.
- He also identifies **את + רע = 671** (401 + 270).

Classification must remain separated:

- existence/identity of the 30-word Masoretic set: **SOURCE CLAIM / CITED SET**.
- 30 × 2 = 60: **CALCULATION**.
- 1…60 = 1,830: **CALCULATION**; simple arithmetic independently checked.
- set gematria = 2,501: **SOURCE CALCULATION; NEEDS CANONICAL ENGINE VERIFICATION**.
- Adonai filling = 671: **SOURCE CALCULATION; NEEDS CANONICAL ENGINE VERIFICATION**.
- 1,830 + 671 = 2,501: **CALCULATION**; arithmetic independently checked.
- את + רע = 671: **SOURCE GEMATRIA / CALCULATION; NEEDS CANONICAL ENGINE VERIFICATION** even though the arithmetic reading is straightforward.
- the significance of all routes converging: **INTERPRETATION**.

Method significance:

**INDEPENDENT-LOOKING DATA/CONSTRUCTION PATHS → SHARED TARGET NUMBER → INTERPRETIVE CONVERGENCE.**

This is the clearest source example so far of what we are provisionally calling **multi-path convergence**. The phrase is our research label, not Horowitz's terminology.

Open work: visually transcribe the full 30-word set; verify that the cited Masoretic population is exactly represented; then run the relevant gematria/milui values only through the canonical live engine/registry before any `engine_verified` claim.

## 5. Horowitz method inventory — current inference

The following is **our research inference from repeated source behavior**, not terminology used by Horowitz himself:

- **COUNT** — count defined units.
- **CLASSIFY** — assign units to semantic/source classes.
- **ORDER** — preserve Torah occurrence order.
- **BOUND** — define start/end of the measured span.
- **AGGREGATE** — parasha → book → Torah totals.
- **DECOMPOSE** — break totals into auditable subcounts.
- **NORMALIZE / ELIGIBILITY** — decide what belongs in the population.
- **COUNTING REGIME** — make inclusion/exclusion/read-vs-written rules explicit.
- **READ/WRITE RESOLUTION** — distinguish written form from traditional reading.
- **IDENTITY MERGE** — multiple mentions → one entity.
- **IDENTITY SPLIT** — identical written form → semantically different unit.
- **POSITIONAL ANALYSIS** — first/middle/last inside a defined population.
- **COMPARE PARALLELS** — compare parallel passages/corpora.
- **RECONCILE** — propose an interpretive model for apparent numerical contradiction.
- **CROSS-CHECK / AUDIT TRAIL** — expose subcounts so a reader can locate divergence.
- **MULTI-PATH CONVERGENCE** — preserve separate paths that reach the same target number rather than collapsing them into one finding.

Working higher-level inference:

Horowitz often appears to operate as:

**define population → resolve identity/eligibility → count → decompose → normalize where needed → compare → interpret.**

A second recurring pattern is now visible:

**source/corpus count → independent calculation or second count → shared numerical target → interpretation.**

This remains a hypothesis until the full book pass tests whether the same pattern holds beyond the first quantitative sections.

## 6. Evidence-strength ranking used in this research

This is a working research ranking, not a canonical SOD1820 law:

- **A — Corpus convergence:** independently defined corpus measurements converge on the same number.
- **B — Normalized corpus convergence:** convergence appears after explicit inclusion/exclusion/masoretic normalization; requires rule-independence audit.
- **C — Positional corpus structure:** first/middle/last or other position inside a reproducibly defined population.
- **D — Constructed numerical correspondence:** selected phrases, gematria constructions, arithmetic arrangements; preserve but rank below corpus measurements until independently justified.
