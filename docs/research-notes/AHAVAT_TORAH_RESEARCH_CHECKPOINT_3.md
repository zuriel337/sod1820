## B. Textual boundary state as research data

At the Vayigash→Vayechi transition Horowitz treats the absence of a parasha break as meaningful textual structure and interprets the continuity through Judah/Joseph unity.

Truth separation:

- presence/absence of a textual/parasha boundary: **STRUCTURAL TEXT OBSERVATION / SOURCE-LOCATED**;
- Judah/Joseph meaning assigned to that boundary state: **SOURCE INTERPRETATION**.

Method label (our terminology):

**TEXTUAL BOUNDARY STATE — boundary exists / boundary absent → compare adjacent semantic units → interpretation.**

This extends BOUND: the boundary itself can be a datum, not only a start/end coordinate for a count.

## F. Item-membership dispute vs aggregate claim

Horowitz explicitly says of his Torah roots table that it contains 1,820 roots and that, in his view, objections may be raised about individual particulars while `the total will not change` (`בפרטות ... והכלל לא ישונה`).

Classification:

- 1,820 roots: **AUTHOR_COUNT / SOURCE CLAIM**;
- stability of the total under disputes over particulars: **AUTHOR'S METHODOLOGICAL CLAIM**, not an independently demonstrated invariant.

Method/governance distinction:

**ITEM MEMBERSHIP CONFIDENCE ≠ AGGREGATE CLAIM.**

Do not infer that the total is objectively invariant merely because Horowitz asserts it.

## I. Method inventory delta after Checkpoint 2

New/clarified method labels justified by this pass (our research terminology):

- **SPEECH ATTRIBUTION METRIC** — attributed spoken words are a distinct metric from mentions/association.
- **TEXTUAL BOUNDARY STATE** — presence/absence of a textual division is itself data.
- **IDENTITY-DRIVEN RECOUNT** — external identity resolution can alter membership and downstream totals.
- **ORDINAL RANGE SUM / TRIANGULAR AGGREGATION** — sum the ordered positions in a defined sequence.
- **CALENDAR / RITUAL SEQUENCE STRUCTURE** — a ritual sequence can define the ordered range used by a calculation.
- **BOUNDED EXTERNAL CORPUS** — apply the same boundary/subsection/count pattern to a non-Torah corpus.
- **ITEM-MEMBERSHIP vs AGGREGATE CLAIM** — preserve uncertainty/dispute at item level separately from an author's global-total assertion.

These extend the existing ledger; they do not create a new engine, store, graph or truth lifecycle.
