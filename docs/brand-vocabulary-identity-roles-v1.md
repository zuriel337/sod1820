This document normalizes naming and role language. It does not create a new identity system, membership system, researcher system, author system, lens engine, or premium hierarchy.

## 1. Core brand relationship

- **SOD1820** is the site and primary system name.
- **כי לה׳ המלוכה** is the core spiritual/public brand identity that has always lived together with SOD1820.
- They are not competing brands and must not be presented as separate products.
- Together they represent the main identity and overwhelming majority of the site experience.
- The primary visual direction remains futuristic, innovative, spatial/cosmic and research-forward even when using sacred/royal symbolism such as the crown.

English projection remains:

- `KINGDOM RISE`
- canonical explanatory line: `For the Kingdom Is the Lord’s`
- same Master Crown across Hebrew and English.

## 2. User / member / researcher / writer are separate axes

Do not collapse these into one progression ladder.

### Account state

- **משתמש** = person using the site who is not registered/signed in.
- **חבר** = registered account holder.

A חבר is not automatically a researcher or writer.

### Research identity

- **חוקר** = a person who has a researcher page/file and participates in the governed research identity system.
- Researcher ranks/levels exist independently from account membership.
- A user/member becomes a researcher only through the actual researcher path; registration alone does not grant researcher identity.

### Writing identity

- **כתב** = a persistent writing/contributor identity.
- **כתב אורח** = a bounded guest contributor identity used when appropriate for a specific post/contribution.
- A writer does not need to have a researcher file.
- A researcher is not automatically a writer.

### Premium / access identity

- **היכל** remains the name of the premium world/product.
- **בן ההיכל / בת ההיכל** = singular membership identity.
- **בני ההיכל** = plural/community identity.
- English brand form: **Bnei HaHeichal**.
- On first explanatory English use, optional: `Bnei HaHeichal · Members of the Heichal`.
- `Heichal` remains transliterated as a product/world name; do not routinely replace it with Palace/Temple/Hall.
- A future premium tier above Bnei HaHeichal is acknowledged but **UNNAMED / UNDECIDED**. Do not invent or ship a public name until Human Gate decides its meaning and benefits.

## 3. Public author/byline language

Canonical default system byline remains:

**מערכת כי לה׳ המלוכה**

Rules:

- New and legacy posts written by the core system default to `מערכת כי לה׳ המלוכה` unless a real different writer identity applies.
- Do not use the private/family-name form for the system byline.
- If another persistent writer authored the post, display that writer.
- If the contribution is bounded/guest, use `כתב אורח · [name]` where the projection supports it.
- If the system materially edited/processed content written by someone else, editorial credit may be separate from author identity; do not collapse source, author, editor and AI provenance.
- `בדרך לנצח` remains a distinct writer identity for relevant `מימד חמש` material.

## 4. Zuriel public identity

Public-facing personal identity should use:

**צוריאל**

Do not default to the family name in new public brand copy.

Recommended About-context forms:

- `צוריאל · ממייסדי SOD1820 וכי לה׳ המלוכה`

Posts written under the core system continue to use `מערכת כי לה׳ המלוכה`; personal identity and system byline are distinct.

## 6. Research/gematria discovery language

The main brand may be simultaneously:

- sacred/royal,
- futuristic/cosmic,
- innovative,
- research-oriented,
- spatial/technological.

Do not force a false split where research language belongs only to a secular lens. Terms such as research, source, finding, method, calculation, cipher, pattern, graph, connection and evidence belong naturally inside the main SOD1820 / כי לה׳ המלוכה experience.

A concise public description may use:

> דרך מחקר המחברת בין מספרים, גימטריה, צפנים, טקסטים ואירועים כדי לבחון דפוסים וקשרים לאורך המציאות.

Historical galleries, posts and visual findings serve as the documented archive of how this method evolved over time.

## 7. `קוד המציאות` — deferred audience lens

`קוד המציאות` exists as a possible audience/content lens under SOD1820, but its future scope is intentionally **NOT LOCKED**.

Current guardrails only:

- It is not a second database, research engine, truth system or canonical graph.
- If used, it consumes the same SOD1820 research infrastructure and provenance.
- Its logo concept may reuse the same Master Crown with the textual lockup `קוד המציאות`, but this does **not** establish it as an equal primary brand.
- It may eventually provide more secular/universal entry language or content prioritization (e.g. selected Dimension Five, synchronicity, patterns, event/number relationships), but no reader-vs-researcher segmentation, routing, entitlement or content strategy is locked yet.
- Do not implement a reader/researcher split merely because this lens exists.

Decision status: **KNOWN POSSIBILITY · STRATEGY DEFERRED**.
