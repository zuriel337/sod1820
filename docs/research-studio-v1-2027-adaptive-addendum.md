## 1. Invariants preserved from the existing Research Studio contract

Preserve:

- **one Research OS** across all tools and projections;
- Research Context is logical navigation/research state, **not a new DB truth store**;
- stable identity + Universal Finding / typed result boundaries;
- Journey/Research Path as traversal/snapshot over the same identities and Context;
- exact reopen/return as a first-class continuity requirement;
- Lens/Dimension separation: Method remains a Dimension/traceable calculation context, not a second research system;
- 2D/list/layered/spatial renderers may represent the same state without creating separate truth;
- bounded readers, pagination/virtualization/progressive expansion; never dump the whole graph/client dataset;
- deterministic engines calculate; AI interprets/researches/ranks/recommends through governed capabilities; calculation fact != interpretation;
- ELS, Number/Gematria, Topic, Person/Name, Book/source and future adapters reuse the same Research OS primitives;
- Human Gate remains the authority for governed canonical promotion/publication decisions.

## 2. Presentation-specific clauses superseded

The following historical Research Studio wording is no longer a current presentation requirement:

- a fixed `ResearchProvider/ResearchCenter` three-column visual Workspace;
- a fixed left/right/center column count as the only valid Research Studio form;
- the statement that the Number Page must grow only additively **and is never rebuilt or replaced**;
- any interpretation that the existing `EntityPage` visual findings area must remain pixel/structure-stable forever;
- any requirement that all deep tools remain visually embedded in the same non-fullscreen shell.

The semantic owners remain; the renderer may change.

## 4. Research Context Spine

The canonical Research Context adapter remains the continuity seam. Current v1 representable state includes:

- subject;
- selection;
- lens;
- primitive dimensions;
- Journey identity/position;
- locale;
- access projection;
- return target.

Context is not allowed to become a dump of transient UI state.

When a future tool needs exact state that the Context envelope cannot safely carry, store/resolve the durable state under its existing domain owner and put only a stable reference/locator in Context. Do not widen Context into a universal hidden store.

## 6. Heichal and direct tools

Heichal is deep-research mode over the same Research Context, **not a gateway requirement**.

A user may directly enter Books, Number/Phrase, ELS or another first-class product from Google, a saved link or internal navigation. Entering Heichal is an optional escalation to deeper research with the current context already loaded.

Tool outputs return typed state/results to the shared Research OS. They do not silently become canonical truth.

---

## 7. My Workspace

My Workspace composes existing personal owners and durable state. Preserve the product intent from `personal_command_center_law`:

> the user enters to understand **what the next useful step is**, not merely to inspect a profile/settings panel.

It may show research/saved items, Journeys/resume, attention/notifications/messages, contributions, profile/progress, credits/access and preferences without duplicating their canonical stores.

---

## 8. Raziel integration

Raziel is one global companion/controller over the same authorized Research Context.

It may explain, navigate, filter, compare, open/run authorized tools, propose candidates and construct/resume Journeys. It cannot own a second truth store, override access, or perform Human-Gate promotion merely because it can invoke a tool.

Existing `ai-analyze kind=number` and similar AI capabilities are capabilities to absorb into the Raziel experience where appropriate, not separate AI identities that must survive as detached UI cards.

---

## 9. Publication vs Research Intake

One Tree does not mean every creation enters one lifecycle.

- ordinary content publication remains under content/publishing owners;
- social/communication remains under its domain owner;
- eligible research material may enter Research Intake;
- governed promotion uses the existing Truth/Human-Gate owners.

Publishing a Post or Chidush does not automatically make it a Research Object or canonical graph fact.

---

## 10. Language / access readiness

Language is a representation projection over the same identity/Context. Locale switching must preserve subject, Journey, orientation and exact return.

Access dimensions are orthogonal:

- capability availability;
- caller entitlement/authorization;
- object publication/access;
- truth/governance state.

Premium/Deep/Admin may expose greater authorized depth and controls without forking entity identity or creating a Premium-only Research OS.
