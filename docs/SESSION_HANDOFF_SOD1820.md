## Current strategy

SOD1820 is one Research OS: one shared research substrate, one graph/tree, one Human-Gate, many entrances and views. ELS remains one canonical engine; 2D, 3D, Source, Numerical, Graph, Timeline and Experiment are capabilities over shared Research Objects.

North Star: **Maximum useful research / Minimum unnecessary computation.**

Formal capability gate: **Capability → Object → Value → Cost → Coverage → Risk → Decision Impact.**

## New strategic layer

A shared Time Research Layer is planned as a view over shared objects: date candidates, derived dates, Temporal Anchors, reverse-time search and Research Timeline. Numerical scale transforms and leading-digit transforms are explicit transformations, not ordinary equality.

The architecture is intentionally open to future research laws. New laws are versioned, provenance-bearing and gated; candidates are not facts. AI is a Research Navigator with Budget and Challenge Mode, not a canonizer.

## Non-negotiables

- No parallel engine/tree/graph/source of truth.
- No silent search-space expansion.
- No silent loss of findings.
- No candidate → fact promotion by AI.
- No canonical/published status without Human-Gate.
- Preserve finding_id, evidence and provenance across views.
- Preserve old saved matrices and compatibility.
- Device-adaptive compute routing, not a second engine.
