## Current strategic state

SOD1820 is being designed as one Research OS: one graph, one research substrate, one Human-Gate, many views and entrances. ELS is one canonical engine and its matrix/3D/Timeline/Graph/Experiment surfaces are capabilities over shared Research Objects.

The product North Star is:

**Maximum useful research / Minimum unnecessary computation.**

The current formal gate before additional implementation is:

**Capability → Object → Value → Cost → Coverage → Risk → Decision Impact.**

## ELS continuity

The existing ELS work includes the canonical engine, D4 search-space correction, FORMS/Split-Join, State Contract/Work Area, research-path Finding→Axis, matrix display controls, three matrix themes, proximity/score controls, 2D/3D/movie/presentation capabilities, save/share and compatibility testing.

Future roadmap must preserve all existing capabilities and add them through progressive disclosure rather than UI accumulation.

## New strategic layer

A shared Time Research Layer is planned as a view over shared objects, including date candidates, derived dates, Temporal Anchors, reverse-time search and Research Timeline. Numerical scale transformations (such as ×10/÷10) and leading-digit transformations are represented as explicit transformations, not as ordinary equality.

The research architecture must remain open to future laws without engine reconstruction. New laws are versioned, provenance-bearing and gated; candidates are not facts. AI is a Research Navigator with Budget and Challenge Mode, not a canonizer.

## Non-negotiables

- No parallel engine.
- No parallel graph/tree.
- No duplicate source of truth.
- No silent search expansion.
- No silent loss of findings.
- No candidate → fact promotion by AI.
- No canonical/published status without Human-Gate.
- Preserve finding_id, evidence and provenance across views.
- Preserve old saved matrices and compatibility.
- Keep the system mobile-capable through device-adaptive compute routing, not a second engine.
