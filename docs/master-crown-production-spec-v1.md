# SOD1820 — Master Crown Production Spec V1

Status: HUMAN-GATE LOCKED IDENTITY · BRANCH ONLY · NOT MERGED · NOT DEPLOYED

Owner: `SOD1820_DESIGN_CONTRACT_V1.md`

OWNER CHECK: `EXTEND_EXISTING`.

This specification governs the fixed Master Crown identity for the SOD1820 / כי לה׳ המלוכה / KINGDOM RISE brand family. It does not create a second logo registry or design owner.

## 1. Canonical brand identity

Hebrew public brand: **כי לה׳ המלוכה**

English public brand: **KINGDOM RISE**

Canonical English line: **For the Kingdom Is the Lord’s**

System identity: **SOD1820**

All language lockups use the **same Master Crown**. Language changes text projection only; it never creates a competing crown identity.

## 2. Master Crown lock

The Human Gate has locked the future Master Crown identity as the **blue-gold royal crown** selected in the Brand Evolution process.

The following are identity-bearing and must remain recognizable across every approved representation:

- royal crown silhouette as the dominant object;
- deep midnight / royal blue body or velvet field;
- luminous polished gold structural frame;
- blue gemstone family as the principal cool accent;
- vertically emphasized central crown axis / crest;
- premium celestial light treatment compatible with the SOD1820 visual language;
- symmetrical, majestic, non-cartoon visual character;
- clear readability at small icon scale after simplification.

These features are not page-local styling suggestions. They define the persistent visual DNA of the Primary Crown.

The exact production asset file(s) remain `ASSET_MASTER_PENDING` until a clean final 2D master and/or production 3D source is exported and accepted. Asset export may refine geometry and rendering quality, but must not redesign the identity.

## 3. Heritage continuity

The existing `/logo.png` crown remains the official **Heritage Mark**.

It is not deleted, deprecated as a mistake, or treated as an obsolete brand failure. It records approximately fifteen years of continuity across the site and historical video material.

Approved relationship:

`Heritage Crown → controlled evolution → Primary Blue-Gold Crown`

The Heritage Mark may appear in About, archive/history, comeback campaigns, historical-video context, and bounded heritage seals/lockups.

The Primary Crown is the future default digital/product mark once the final master asset is separately released.

## 4. Canonical representation family

One identity may have multiple representations. These are variants, not new logos.

### A. Primary Crown — symbol only
Use for navbar, hero focal mark, app icon source, video sting source, product shell and master brand moments.

### B. Hebrew lockup
Master Crown + `כי לה׳ המלוכה`.
Optional secondary technical mark: `SOD1820`.

### C. English lockup
Master Crown + `KINGDOM RISE`.
Canonical supporting line when space allows: `For the Kingdom Is the Lord’s`.
Optional secondary technical mark: `SOD1820`.

### D. Horizontal lockup
Simplified crown at logical start + localized brand name. Intended for navbar/footer/wide share contexts.

### E. Compact icon
Simplified crown silhouette preserving the central axis and blue-gold identity. Intended for favicon/app icons/small badges. Small-size simplification is allowed; changing the crown identity is not.

### F. Monochrome mark
Single-color silhouette/line treatment for print, embossing, accessibility/high-contrast or constrained production. Geometry must remain recognizable.

### G. Heritage lockup
Primary Crown dominant + bounded Heritage Mark reference, or Heritage Crown with explicit continuity context. Never two equal competing primary logos.

### H. Motion mark
Animated projection of the same Master Crown. Motion can reveal, illuminate, assemble, rotate slightly, breathe light, or transition from Heritage to Primary.

### I. 3D Crown
Spatial/material realization of the same Master Crown. 3D is not permission to invent a different crown.

## 5. Motion law

Approved motion vocabulary:

- Heritage Crown softly resolves into the Primary Crown;
- gold frame traces/assembles from controlled light;
- blue gemstones illuminate in sequence;
- subtle crown depth reveal / small camera arc;
- restrained celestial glow/orbit field;
- optional short brand-line reveal after the crown stabilizes.

Forbidden:

- random swapping between Heritage and Primary in ordinary navigation;
- aggressive spinning, bouncing or game-like animation;
- deformation that changes the crown silhouette;
- perpetual autoplay that competes with reading;
- essential brand recognition depending only on motion.

`prefers-reduced-motion` must receive a static Primary Crown or static Primary + Heritage seal composition.

## 6. 3D production law

A production 3D model may add physically plausible depth, gold metal, dark royal-blue velvet/enamel surfaces, blue gemstones, controlled reflections and cinematic light.

It must preserve:

- front-view silhouette;
- central vertical crest;
- principal gold-to-blue relationship;
- gemstone hierarchy;
- crown proportions sufficiently close that the 2D and 3D versions are unmistakably the same mark.

Allowed animation-ready model preparation:

- separated crown frame / blue body / gemstone materials;
- grouped illumination channels;
- controlled camera-ready pivot/origin;
- clean topology suitable for web/video rendering;
- LOD or simplified mobile version;
- transparent-background render pipeline.

Do not bake text into the 3D crown. Hebrew/English names are separate lockup layers.

## 7. Export family

The final production package should eventually contain, without creating multiple identities:

- transparent 2D Primary Crown master;
- SVG/vector or clean redraw equivalent where technically appropriate;
- high-resolution PNG/WebP transparent exports;
- dark-background and light-background tested renders;
- compact square app icon;
- favicon-safe simplified mark;
- Hebrew horizontal and stacked lockups;
- English horizontal and stacked lockups;
- Heritage seal/lockup;
- monochrome version;
- motion master / source project;
- 3D source model + textures/material definitions + optimized web export when commissioned.

Do not infer that an image-generation preview is automatically the production master file. Production acceptance requires a clean asset export and visual check.

## 8. Usage hierarchy

Primary Crown = normal future digital/product identity.

Heritage Crown = continuity/history identity.

Category/Series Signatures such as Strong Hints, ELS, Sod HaHashmal and Journey remain subordinate signatures. They may never visually replace the Master Crown as the global brand owner.

`KINGDOM RISE` and `כי לה׳ המלוכה` are language projections of the same brand, not parent/child brands.

## 9. Change control

The Master Crown identity is HUMAN-GATE LOCKED.

Do not redesign or replace its visual DNA because of model preference, a new generated image, a new animation vendor, a new renderer or a new language.

Permitted without reopening identity:

- cleanup/redraw;
- vectorization;
- animation;
- 3D modeling;
- material/lighting refinement;
- responsive simplification;
- localization of text lockups;
- technical export adaptation.

Any material change to crown identity/silhouette/color family/brand hierarchy requires a new explicit ZURIEL Human Gate decision.

## 10. Release boundary

This specification does not by itself authorize replacing `/logo.png`, merging PR #410, deploying a new sitewide mark, or publishing a 3D runtime.

Normal release remains required after the production Master Crown asset is selected/exported and verified.
