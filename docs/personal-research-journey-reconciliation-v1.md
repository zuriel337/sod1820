The journey is a Projection of the same Research OS around a Person. It is not a new content type or truth store.

## Engine-agnostic law

Gematria, ELS, Tanach, Numeric, Spatial, Corpus, Timeline and future registered engines are lenses/producers of separate Findings inside one Research Journey.

`Same Question → Multiple Engines → Separate Findings → possible Research Convergence`

Cross-engine convergence does not automatically establish evidence independence and never auto-promotes an Interpretation to Fact/Canonical.

## Research Intent + Research Actions

Where available, preserve why/what the researcher is investigating and whether the search was targeted or the occurrence pre-existing/unsolicited.

Research actions are provenance:

`seen → searched → revisited → saved → collected → connected → interpreted → accepted / rejected / open`

Non-equivalence:

`Search ≠ Belief`

`Save ≠ Approval`

`Behavior ≠ Human-Gate`

Existing ResearchProvider / `research_items` workspace semantics remain owners of workspace membership; this reconciliation creates no new persistence owner.

## Chronology of knowing

Preserve the difference between:
- event/observation time;
- capture time;
- discovery time;
- interpretation time;
- decision time.

Later knowledge must never silently rewrite an earlier research snapshot as though the later meaning was known at the earlier time.

This extends the existing Personal Foundation snapshot/reproducibility requirement and does not compete with `OD-TIME-8`, which remains owner of the broad Time model.

## Lineage + independence

The Research OS must remain able to represent three logical lineage dimensions without creating a new store:

1. **Source Lineage** — where the Finding came from.
2. **Event Lineage** — which Findings originate from the same event/cluster.
3. **Model Lineage** — how a researcher's understanding changes over time.

Laws:

`Anchor Independence ≠ Evidence Independence`

`Row Count ≠ Independent Evidence Count`

Same-source, same-event and derived results must not be silently inflated as independent corroboration. Exact implementation shape remains future work; extend existing refs/meta/provenance first.

## Research History ≠ Research Identity

Research History records what a person searched, saw, saved or did.

Research Identity / Personal Research Anchor describes questions or anchors that become persistent or central over time.

One search does not create an Anchor. Future ranking may consider recurrence, persistence, branch depth, saved Findings, engine diversity and explicit user confirmation, but ranking may only propose significance; it does not canonize it.

## Privacy

Personal Journey may compose private and public source-native material at query time, but Projection does not change visibility.

`STORE ≠ RETRIEVE ≠ PARTICIPATE-IN-RESEARCH ≠ ANALYZE ≠ PROMOTE ≠ PUBLISH`

A private Finding does not become public because it participates in a Personal Journey or Research Convergence.
