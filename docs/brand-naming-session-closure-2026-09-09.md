## World / Heichal / Beit Midrash hierarchy

### העולם / World
Current 2027 architecture defines the World as the shared knowledge reality of SOD1820: entities, relations, findings, topics/convergences, numbers/phrases, persons, books/sources/passages, posts, galleries/images, ELS findings, events/time, journeys, provenance, truth/publication/access state.

Topics and convergences belong to the World architecture; they are not semantically owned by Beit Midrash.

### היכל / Heichal
Heichal = deep research mode/workspace inside the World.
Invariant: **World focus → Heichal → Tool → typed result → World**.
Heichal owns tools/modes, not a separate knowledge graph.

### בית המדרש / Beit Midrash
Beit Midrash remains a learning/explanation/study surface: methods, teaching, lessons, explanatory content and guided study. It is not the future semantic home of all topics/convergences.
