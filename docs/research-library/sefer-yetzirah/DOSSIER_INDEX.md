## Source facts

- **Title:** ספר יצירה המיוחס לאברהם אבינו ע"ה, נסודר בשלימות על פי נוסח הקדוש הארי אשכנזי זל, עם פירוש הגר"א, ונלוה עליו פרי יצחק (Sefer Yetzirah, attributed to Avraham Avinu, with the commentary of the Vilna Gaon and the appended commentary "Peri Yitzchak") — this title-page description applies to the pp.153+ Gra/Peri-Yitzchak section; pp.85-125 (this batch's own range) instead sit inside the book's **earlier 9-commentary synoptic section** (Raavad, Ramban, Saadia-Gaon-attributed, Eleazar of Worms, Botril, Otzar Hashem, per the front title page at p.5, outside this batch's own range).
- **Gra-commentary editor:** שמואל בלאאמ"ס אליעזר אהרן לוריא ז"ל ממאהליב (Shmuel, son of Eliezer Aharon Luria, of Mohilev) — per the title page (p.153) and "הצעת המסדר" (p.154)
- **Second commentary author (Peri Yitzchak):** ר' יצחק אייזיק, אב"ד מאהליב, author of "באר יצחק" on Tikkunei Zohar — his Sefer Yetzirah commentary is titled on the title page "ספר יקר המציאות" but runs under the printed header "פרי יצחק"
- **Printer / imprint:** Warsaw ("ווארשא"); printer "האחים לעוונעפשטיין" / Drukarnia L. Lewin-Epstein, Warszawa (per the task dispatch's file description)
- **Extent:** 220 PDF pages (confirmed via the Read tool's page-count report)
- **Durable source artifact:** `gallery/Book/Sefer Yetsirah.pdf`, canonical Supabase project `linswmnnkjxvweumprav`. Local scratch copy: sha256 `2cc0710667dde3db94745b4f652d4b2e3eb8ea9c64424ab52f1f2ce44281c13a`, 51,714,643 bytes.
- **Physical structure (as mapped across all indexed artifacts so far — pp.1-84, 126-152, and 208-220 remain UNMAPPED by this dossier):**
  - **pp.85-125 (mapped by artifact #2, this batch):** Chapter 2 Mishnah 5-7 continuation (tzaruf/shakal/hemir letter-pairing procedure + 22-row alphabet-pairing table, p.85; combinatorics continuing to p.90) → Chapter 3 (three mothers אמ"ש) opens p.91, runs to ~p.98 → Chapter 4 (seven doubles) opens p.99, systematic per-letter coronation formula through p.111-112 (including the "houses from stones" factorial-combinatorics passage), closes with colophon "סליק פירקא" at p.112 → Chapter 5 (twelve simples) opens p.113, runs to p.120, closes with the celebrated 22-letter (3+7+12) recapitulation Mishnah and its own "סליק פירקא" → Chapter 6 opens p.121 (three-mothers combinatorics, climaxing in the Abraham Avinu creation-narrative at p.123), and the **entire synoptic 9-commentary edition closes at p.124** with the double colophon "סליק פרקא, וסליק ספר יצירה" → **p.125 is Sefer HaChachmoni's own title page** ("ספר חכמוני. פירוש על ספר יצירה, להרב הקדמון רבי שבתי דונולו ז"ל..."), confirmed via direct single-page verification. A circular diagram is anchored exactly at p.102 (Ch.4, seven-doubles cosmology). The literal phrase "רל"א שערים" does NOT appear anywhere in pp.85-125.
  - **p.126 onward through p.152 (still UNMAPPED by any artifact in this dossier as of artifact #2):** artifact #2 glanced at p.126 once, strictly to confirm the p.124/125/126 transition boundary (confirming the Donolo-biography-via-Buber + "דברים אחדים"/Castelli-preface material sits at p.126, AFTER the p.125 title page, not before it as a naive reading of an earlier, unrelated register might suggest) — this is boundary evidence only, not a registered extraction of p.126's own content. pp.128-152 (the Chachmoni treatise body) remain fully unmapped by this dossier.
  - pp.153-154: title page + "הצעת המסדר" (the Arranger's Presentation, an introduction — NOT titled "דעת הספר")
  - pp.155-195: פירוש הגר"א — the Gra's commentary on Sefer Yetzirah's 6 chapters, cycling through all Mishnayot, with a parallel "פרי יצחק" commentary column on every page; closes with an explicit colophon on p.195 ("סליק פירוש הגר"א")
  - pp.196-202: Hadran (siyum formula) + "ליקוטי הגר"א ז"ל לספר יצירה" (Likutei HaGra — gleanings), closing with "תם ונשלם" on p.202
  - p.203: "סדר הרל"א שערים לפי דעת הגר"א ז"ל" — the Gra's 231-Gates combinatorial table (the literal "231 Gates" phrase IS confirmed here — contrast with pp.85-125 above, where it is confirmed ABSENT)
  - p.204: a geometric letter-diagram (12 Simple Letters on a sefirotic lattice) + start of a second, independent 231-Gates table attributed to Peri Yitzchak
  - pp.205-206: Peri Yitzchak's 231-Gates tables, organized by sefirah/partzuf (Keter/Chochmah/Binah/Da'at observed)
  - p.207 onward (p.207 is artifact #1's edge): "ל"ב נתיבות מבעל פרי יצחק" (The 32 Paths, by Peri Yitzchak) begins
  - **p.208 onward (UNMAPPED):** an "Ari commentary" is expected to begin around here — NOT independently verified by any artifact in this dossier, as pp.208-220 have not yet been read by anything indexed here.
