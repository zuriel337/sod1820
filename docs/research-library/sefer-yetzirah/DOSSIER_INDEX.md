## Source facts

- **Title:** ספר יצירה המיוחס לאברהם אבינו ע"ה, נסודר בשלימות על פי נוסח הקדוש הארי אשכנזי זל, עם פירוש הגר"א, ונלוה עליו פרי יצחק (Sefer Yetzirah, attributed to Avraham Avinu, with the commentary of the Vilna Gaon and the appended commentary "Peri Yitzchak") — this specific title-page description is printed at p.153 and applies most directly to the pp.153+ Gra/Peri-Yitzchak section. The earlier pp.5-152 span is its own **9-commentary synoptic section** (Raavad, Ramban, Saadia-Gaon-attributed, Eleazar of Worms, Botril, Otzar Hashem, per that section's own front title page at p.5) followed by Sefer HaChachmoni (R' Shabtai Donolo)'s independent treatise. This is one bound volume containing multiple originally-separate works, not a single continuous commentary.
- **Gra-commentary editor:** שמואל בלאאמ"ס אליעזר אהרן לוריא ז"ל ממאהליב (Shmuel, son of Eliezer Aharon Luria, of Mohilev) — per the title page (p.153) and "הצעת המסדר" (p.154).
- **Second commentary author (Peri Yitzchak):** ר' יצחק אייזיק, אב"ד מאהליב, author of "באר יצחק" on Tikkunei Zohar — his Sefer Yetzirah commentary is titled on the title page "ספר יקר המציאות" but runs under the printed header "פרי יצחק".
- **Printer / imprint:** Warsaw ("ווארשא"); Drukarnia L. Lewin-Epstein / "האחים לעווין-עפשטיין", per the front title page (p.5/6) and the printer's stamp at p.4.
- **Extent:** 220 PDF pages (confirmed independently by multiple artifacts' page-count reads).
- **Durable source artifact:** `gallery/Book/Sefer Yetsirah.pdf`, canonical Supabase project `linswmnnkjxvweumprav`. Scratch-copy sha256 `2cc0710667dde3db94745b4f652d4b2e3eb8ea9c64424ab52f1f2ce44281c13a`, 51,714,643 bytes (confirmed identical across independent sessions).
- **Excluded object:** `gallery/Book/Hebrewbooks_org_33050.pdf` is a **different work** (Ma'amar Esther, R' Shlomo Kluger, Brody 1912) and is not part of this dossier under any circumstance.

### Physical structure (reconciled across all six artifacts; page numbers are PDF page numbers unless marked "printed p.")

- **pp.1-4:** front cover + blank flyleaves (photographic scan).
- **p.5:** printed title page naming the 9 commentaries of the synoptic section.
- **p.6:** Warsaw/Lewin-Epstein imprint page.
- **p.7:** Grodno haskama (dated 1806, a reprinted historical document) + opening of הקדמת הראב"ד ז"ל.
- **pp.8-27:** Raavad's hakdama.
- **p.28:** a separate one-page hakdama insert by R' Moshe Botril.
- **pp.29 onward:** the main synoptic interlaced body begins, this edition's own Chapter 1 Mishnah 1. **This edition's own Chapter/Mishnah numbering diverges from the standard short-recension numbering** (confirmed by artifact #4): its Chapter 1 runs to at least Mishnah 13, restating the 3-mothers/7-doubles/12-simples formula once mid-Chapter-1 (its own M.10, p.65) and again as the opening of its own Chapter 2 (p.76, immediately after a "סליק פרקא" colophon). Named Research-DNA hits in this span: letter-permutation procedure (Ch.2 M.2, p.79); the five places of articulation, **with a printed footnote citing the Mantua edition's textual variant for this exact Mishnah** (Ch.2 M.3, p.80); the 231 gates as primary Mishnah text (Ch.2 M.4, p.80, same page); a zodiac/angel-name table and a 12-permutation Tetragrammaton wheel diagram (pp.40, 42).
- **p.85:** the tzaruf/shakal/hemir letter-pairing Mishnah (Ch.2 M.5) verbatim, a full digit-by-digit combination-count enumeration, and a 22-row alphabet-pairing table (headed "והנה אכתוב האלפא ביתא" — this page does **not** use the phrase "231 gates"; that phrase is confirmed present only at p.203, see below).
- **pp.85-90:** combinatorics continuation.
- **p.91:** Chapter 3 (three mothers, אמ"ש) opens.
- **p.99:** Chapter 4 (seven doubles) opens; systematic per-letter formula through pp.111-112 including a "houses from stones" factorial-combinatorics passage; closes with "סליק פירקא" at p.112. A circular diagram is anchored exactly at p.102.
- **p.113:** Chapter 5 (twelve simples) opens, runs to p.120, closes with the 22-letter (3+7+12) recapitulation Mishnah and its own "סליק פירקא".
- **p.121:** Chapter 6 opens (three-mothers combinatorics), climaxing in the Abraham Avinu creation-narrative at p.123.
- **p.124:** the **entire synoptic 9-commentary edition closes here** with the double colophon "סליק פרקא, וסליק ספר יצירה" (per artifact #2's direct verification — this **corrects** artifact #3's original coarse estimate that this boundary fell around p.125/126).
- **p.125:** Sefer HaChachmoni's own title page ("ספר חכמוני. פירוש על ספר יצירה, להרב הקדמון רבי שבתי דונולו ז"ל...").
- **p.126:** editorial insert — a Donolo biography (quoting R' Shlomo Buber's introduction to Lekach Tov) + "דברים אחדים", a preface by Dr. David Castelli of Florence.
- **p.127:** Chachmoni's own hakdama, in Donolo's first-person voice.
- **pp.128-134:** חלק ראשון (Part One — the "Na'aseh Adam"/microcosm philosophical preamble), including a "דמהו לעולם" microcosm sub-section (pp.131-134) and an embryology-as-ruach/mayim/eish exposition (pp.133-134). Closes at the top of p.135.
- **p.135:** חלק שני (Part Two — the actual verse-by-verse Sefer Yetzirah commentary) begins, explicitly headed "זה תחלת פירוש ספר יצירה...".
- **Within Part Two (per artifact #5's direct reading, which corrects artifact #3's coarser estimate):** Ch.1 (ten sefirot), pp.136-138; **Ch.2 (22 letters), pp.138-139** — including the batch's highest-value find, a direct restatement of the five places of articulation at p.138 ("ואלו הן חקוקות בקול, חצובות ברוח, קבועות בפה, בחמשה מקומות..."); Ch.3 (three mothers), pp.140-141, with an explicit עולם/שנה/נפש co-occurrence at p.140; **Ch.4 (seven doubles + a long planetary/astronomical excursus), pp.141-148**; **Ch.5 (twelve simples), pp.148-150** — **artifact #3's original coarse register misidentified p.148 as "Chapter Six"; it is Chapter Five** (opening "ספר שלישי של שנים עשר ההלוחיות הנקראות פשוטות"); **Ch.6 actually begins at p.150** (not p.148 as artifact #3's coarse pass estimated), running to p.151+ with the Teli/Galgal/Lev ruling-triad formula and a citation of Isaiah 57:15.
- **p.152:** Sefer HaChachmoni treatise closes with an editor/compiler colophon (out of scope for artifacts #4 and #5; owned by artifact #3).
- **pp.153-154:** title page + "הצעת המסדר" (the Arranger's Presentation — **not** "דעת הספר", correcting an earlier hypothesis).
- **pp.155-195:** פירוש הגר"א, the Vilna Gaon's commentary cycling through all 6 chapters, with a parallel "פרי יצחק" column on every page; closes with "סליק פירוש הגר"א" at p.195.
- **pp.196-202:** Hadran + "ליקוטי הגר"א ז"ל לספר יצירה", closing "תם ונשלם" at p.202. (Explicit negative finding: no piyutim and no "מאמר קדישין" anywhere in pp.196-207 — contrary to an earlier loose hypothesis for this zone.)
- **p.203:** "סדר הרל"א שערים לפי דעת הגר"א ז"ל" — the literal phrase "231 gates" IS confirmed here (contrast pp.85-125, where it is confirmed absent).
- **p.204:** a geometric letter-diagram (12 simple letters on a sefirotic lattice) + start of a second, independent 231-gates table attributed to Peri Yitzchak.
- **pp.205-206:** Peri Yitzchak's 231-gates tables, organized by sefirah/partzuf (Keter/Chochmah/Binah/Da'at observed; continuation beyond Da'at not determined).
- **p.207 onward:** "ל"ב נתיבות מבעל פרי יצחק" (the 32 Paths) begins.
- **pp.208-211:** פירוש מהאר"י הקדוש זצ"ל (Ari-attributed commentary) — **per artifact #6, this corrects an earlier estimate of "pp.208-212"**: it runs from the bottom half of p.208 through p.211 only, closing with its own decorative printer's ornament.
- **p.212:** a previously-uncounted insert page, half-title "כליל תכנית" ("this is Ma'amar Kadishin from the Ari..."), containing a **first, distinct instance** each of Sefer Amash and Sefer Bagad Kafrat, plus a 12-simple-letters path list.
- **p.213:** Maamar Kadishin proper + R' Yaakov Tamarlish's explanation, plus a **second, textually distinct instance** of Sefer Amash / Sefer Bagad Kafrat. **The p.212 and p.213 instances differ in wording and must never be merged** (a genuine textual-variant finding, preserved as `_v1`/`_v2` in artifact #6's register).
- **pp.214-216: the "old recension" (נוסחא ישנה), with its start point corrected by artifact #6.** A large editorial banner at **p.214** (not p.216 as an earlier boundary estimate had it) reads, verbatim: *"לשלמות המלאכה והדפסתי גם נוסח הספר יצירה כפי שהוא מסודר בדפוס מנטובה (שנת שכ"ב) בסופו, אשר עליו קבעו המפרשים הראשונים פירושיהם: ה"ה ר' שבתי דונולו, הראב"ד, רס"ג, ור"א מגרמיזא, ועוד"* — this is **printed, in-source evidence** for the "Mantua 1562" identification (recorded as a source-printed editorial claim, not independent codicological verification against a surviving exemplar). The recension's own text then runs pp.214→215→216, closing at **p.216** with "סליק ספר יצירה הקדוש, בעזר שמו יתברך ויתעלה", immediately followed by the volume's own printer/compositor colophon (ר' גרשון הלוי מוויכאליסקי מווילנא).
- **pp.217-219:** blank rear flyleaves. **p.220:** back cover (photographic scan).
