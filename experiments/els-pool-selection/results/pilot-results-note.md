# EXP-12 pilot — results note (28.10.2026; descriptive, small n, no test statistic is claimed)

Plan executed as reviewed: 22 requests (plan-review.json sha256 `fe41b8d8…c280e`, commit 0ce0155af), Message Batches through the request-locked temporary pass-through, one request per run, **no retries, no failed or unusable call** (22/22 `end_turn`, parsed). Wall clock about 25 minutes (each batch 1.7-4 minutes). **Actual cost USD 2.826** (pilot cap 8; expected 5.4; worst case 8.0); total spent 31.549 of 40, remaining 8.451. Per run: `controls/summary.md`; every original proposal with its verdict: `results/proposals.md`; raw answers, requests, preflight and engine checks: `controls/runs/<id>/`.
Blind arms (B1, B2, B3, F) and diagnostic arms (D1, D2) are reported apart. Pool-creation failures are not model failures.

## Question 1 — are the two known words in the candidate pool? (offline, before any call)
**No.** `אירנ`, `ארהב` (and `סיכנ`, `ארהבאששבת`) are not in the word list: pool-creation failure (details in `offline-q1-q2.md`). In the blind arms the models therefore could not have shown them. They were added as ordinary items only in the diagnostic pool D1.

## Question 2 — is their geometric relation represented correctly?
Yes in D1 (relation R0103, `same_line`, gap 2, listed in the packet as `R0103|אותו קו|F069|F173|רווח 2`), equal to the independent computation. They are **not parallel**: same column, 10-letter ELS `אירנויארהב` at skip 10101. The registered parallel limit (6 columns) hid the relation of `אירנ` to the axis (13 columns; shares 4 of the axis' 7 rows). Post-hoc count (`review/axis-parallels.json`): in the real window and in each of the 12 tiles, 52-87 word-list words of 4+ letters run alongside the axis with ≥ 4 shared rows (real window 70, 9 of them starting on the axis' first row in the same direction; tiles 4-13 such), so "a word beside the axis" is not rare by itself.

## Question 3 — does the model choose to show them together, and say why? (D1, diagnostic)
**No, in 3 of 3 runs** (Sonnet ×2, Fable ×1): no group holds an `אירנ` item and an `ארהב` item, and `אירנ` (both occurrences) and `סיכנ` were not used anywhere (groups, singles, phrases, continuations). The `same_line` relation between them was in the packet. What the models did pick from the added items: the 9-letter `ארהבאששבת` (E ≈ 5·10⁻⁶, the lowest E in the pool) in all three runs as the rarest finding; Sonnet once paired it with `ארהב` (nested, same line), Fable grouped `ארהב` (K1) + `ארהבאששבת` + the vertical `ארהב` and named the shared cell. Selection followed E (rarity) and overlap, not the "words side by side" structure. With the structure hint (D2, Sonnet ×1, pool without the added words) the number of groups holding a parallel pair rose from 3-5 to 10: the hint steers the choice (diagnostic only).

## Question 4 — rank, and how many similar groups do control windows give?
* Registered pair score: `same_line` is not a scored type, so the pair has no rank; the parallel pair `ארהב`∥`סיכנ` is rank 517 of 560 ELS pairs.
* Pool level (offline): every one of the 12 tiles holds a tight group of word-list words at least as large as the known {אירנ, ארהב, סיכנ}.
* Model level (Sonnet, ×N): groups holding a registered PP pair per answer: real region 3.8 (n=5: B1 4.0, B2 3.5), control tiles 0.5 (n=6); tight (post-hoc) groups 3.4 vs 1.0. Fable: real 2.5 (n=2). Haiku: real 5 (n=1), tiles 2 (n=2). The pool-level PP counts are similar (real 75; tiles 41-107), so this difference between answers on the real region and on tiles is **unexplained**, small-n, and should not be read as evidence about the structure until it is repeated on more tiles.

## Question 5 — free proposals with no story, no topic and no list (arm F, blind)
Sonnet: 20 proposals, 18 verified, **all 18 are runs of the plain text row, none an ELS**, 2 not found. Haiku: 12 proposals, 12 verified, 11 plain text and one ELS (the axis phrase itself). **None of the saved words was proposed.** Without a word list these two models, reading the 31 × 41 window, did not find a single new ELS word. (The Fable call of EXP-16 for the same window ended at max_tokens and is not repeated.)
Phrases and continuations proposed on the pool (all pool arms): 71 phrases, of which 60 passed the local check and were **all confirmed by the engine**; the other 11 were not on one line in reading order (Fable 10, Haiku 1; recorded as `phrase_not_contiguous`, not repaired, not sent to the engine). 53 continuations, 49 confirmed; 4 used letters that were not the letters shown (`continuation_letters_not_shown`: Haiku 3, Sonnet 1) and were not confirmed.

## How the models behave (description)
* **Sonnet 5.5** (adaptive; it used no thinking tokens in any run): 9-12 groups of 2-9 findings, almost always connected by computed relations (share 0.91-1); about half of the group members have E ≥ 1 (chance-level words) and some answers read a high E as support although the packet says it is noise; chains of the plain text row (e.g. a verse) are proposed as "continuations". Runs on the same pool repeat only partly (Jaccard of the findings used 0.59-0.71 in B1/B2, 0.44 in D1; the Jaccard values listed for B3 compare different tiles and mean nothing).
* **Fable 5.1** (thinking 2.4-12.8K tokens): prefers rare ELS findings in the blind arms (E ≥ 1 share 0.14-0.24; 0.48 in D1) and builds meaning fields ("ברית, הבאה, קרבן שלמים") from crossings with text rows; more validator errors (18-39 per answer, 10-16 not about digits: ids in two groups, non-contiguous phrases, relations outside the group), cost 0.46-0.73 per run.
* **Haiku 5.5**: cheapest (0.004), thinking 0-3.9K tokens, 54 errors in the one real run (30 other than digits).
* Digit errors are mostly counts copied from the relation lines or family labels ("4 cells", "K4"); `errors_other_than_digits` separates them.

## What this does and does not show
The pilot shows that, as built, the system (word-list pool + computed relations + a model choosing groups) **does not identify the pair as a structure**: the pair is missing from the pool for list reasons; when it is forced into the pool the models do not choose it; free proposals do not reach it; and the geometry alone (collinear words, a word beside the axis) is produced by every control window. It does **not** show that the structure is chance, nor that it is not meaningful: the strength that a reader sees (Iran, USA, "danger", Israel around "uranium") is semantic, and neither the pool nor the models measured it. A negative result is about these settings (recipe, prompt, models), not about the research direction.

## Limits and what may have been dropped
Word-list coverage (names, abbreviations, spelling variants), line families beyond the 11 registered, region bounds, the shortlist cut (300), words of 4-9 letters, the packet relation filter (parallels beyond 3 cells and near pairs with a 4-letter member are not listed), E treats letters as independent, control tiles are neighbours in the same text, n is 1-6 per cell, one pool per window, reasons are interpretation, validator strictness (digits). Post-hoc measures (tight groups, collinear pairs, axis-parallel counts) were defined after the pair was seen.
