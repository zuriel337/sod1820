## 1. Convergences and Crossings are one public research family

Human Gate decision:

- Do **not** create two parallel product destinations called `התכנסויות` and `הצלבות`.
- `התכנסות` is the primary display/composition unit.
- `הצלבה` is a typed research fact/relationship that helps explain why a convergence is strong.
- A convergence may contain multiple crossings, methods, sources, events, ELS results, Findings or other typed research support.
- A single interesting crossing may remain a Finding/relationship without automatically minting a durable Convergence identity.
- Legacy Topic/Convergence pages remain compatibility/provenance input only; future semantics are governed by One Reality + Result/Research composition.

Example calibration:

`ירושלים = שומרים` is presented as a strong convergence. Its explanation can expose four verified calculation crossings:

- regular: 596
- gadol: 1156
- square: 2650
- large square: 3210

The convergence is one research composition; the four crossings are not four duplicate product objects.

## 2. World is the shared semantic workspace

World answers: **what do we know, what is connected, and why?**

Heichal answers: **what do I want to do now with the selected research context?**

Therefore World may project:

- ranked convergences;
- qualified identities/anchors;
- typed relations / Explain Why;
- Findings + Evidence + Sources;
- time/evolution/history;
- media/representations;
- Dynamic Lenses;
- Research Pulse / what is new or changed;
- Journey entry / exact continuation;
- renderers such as Tree / Graph / Timeline / List / Visual-Spatial.

These are projections of one shared Reality, not independent truth systems.

## 3. Default World convergence presentation

Default public experience direction:

- show a small **Top Convergences** set first (working default: about 5, presentation count remains Experience-owned);
- provide `כל ההתכנסויות` for deeper exploration;
- filters/lenses may include strong, new, curated, multi-method, numeric, ELS, sources, events and contextual/personal views where authorized;
- exact card count, layout, motion and responsive composition are deferred to Projection/Experience.

The Top set is dynamic and explainable. It is not a hard-coded canonical ranking list.

## 4. 2029 convergence ranking policy

A World convergence rank must not be `meter_score` alone.

Candidate signals include, where actually available and authorized:

1. Human curation / explicit editorial importance;
2. exact engine verification / method trace quality;
3. multi-method or cross-capability research strength;
4. evidence dependency/independence classification;
5. source/witness quality and provenance;
6. contradiction/control state;
7. contextual relevance to current Anchor/Lens/Journey;
8. novelty / information gain / meaningful temporal emergence;
9. reusable/durable Research Structure value where qualified;
10. legacy `meter_score` or signal volume as one explainable signal only.

Hard rules:

- Rank != Truth.
- Signal != Curated.
- Match count != authority.
- Repeated representations/derivations must not inflate strength.
- Access/privacy/publication filters happen before ranking.
- Automatic discovery is not automatically featured.
- Human Gate may promote/prioritize without changing mathematical or epistemic truth.

## 5. Legacy meter calibration

Current Explorer can rank legacy public topic/convergence rows by `meter_score`, but that is a transitional display signal, not the final 2029 ranking contract.

Known calibration problem:

- an auto-generated gap-fill with many same-method phrases can receive a high meter;
- a curated four-method convergence such as `ירושלים = שומרים` can have the same meter;
- the 2029 World must distinguish those cases using curation, method diversity, verification, provenance and evidence structure rather than raw volume.

Auto-generated `gapfill-*` rows therefore remain valuable discovery/signal material but should not outrank curated multi-method research merely because of phrase count.
