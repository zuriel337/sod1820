## Human-Gate decision

A strong / contextually qualified Research Anchor or Constellation may justify a bounded expansion into weaker secondary hint operators. Weak transforms do not run globally and cannot create primary significance by themselves.

## Canonical owner

`research_strategy_layer_law` v12 is the active Research Strategy owner. v11 remains preserved as inactive history.

v12 preserves all prior Research Strategy semantics and adds:

- Primary Anchor / qualified Constellation → bounded contextual expansion → secondary hint operators.
- Anchor Strength controls search radius / eligibility for secondary exploration; it does not confer truth.
- Secondary hints cannot inherit primary evidentiary significance merely because they were discovered near a strong Anchor.
- Weak operators require bounded budgets and anti-noise discipline.
- Search-space / selection provenance must remain explicit so post-hoc exploration cannot masquerade as pre-registered evidence.
- Dependency grouping remains required before ranking so repeated/dependent secondary results do not inflate strength.
- Explain-Why must state that a secondary result was surfaced because of a qualified primary context.
- Public/product language must distinguish primary convergence/evidence from contextual secondary hints.
- These semantics apply across Research OS domains; no separate hint graph/store/engine is created.
